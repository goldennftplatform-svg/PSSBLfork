Timeline

  • 2008: The Adirondack is formed with three teams: The Generals, the Osprey, and the Bees.
  • 2009: The division expands to 5 teams with the addition of the Wizards and the Mustangs.
  • 2010: The Solar Sox join the division to bring the total teams to 6. The teams play a 16 game schedule.
  • 2011: Adirondack becomes an 8 team division with the addition of the Timbers and the Silver Foxes (who came over from the Teton Division).
  • 2012: The Silver Foxes and Timbers move up to the newly formed Everest Division, leaving Adirondack with 6 teams once more.
  • 2014: The Mustangs are moved to the Everest Division. The remaining 5 teams move to an 18 game schedule.
  • 2016: A new expansion team, the Mavericks, join the division.
  • 2017: As a one year transitional team, a new Twins team joins the division.
  • 2018: The Twins leave the division for the newly formed Rainier Division.
  • 2019: The Osprey go undefeated and finish 21-0.
  • 2020: Season cancelled due to Covid.
  • 2021: The division schedule drops down to 16 games.
  • 2022: An 18 game schedule is restored.
