Last year the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League created a midweek, midday baseball series for players Age 65 & Over.  This innovative baseball experience was so popular, the League moved to expand the opportunities for veteran players to participate in the game they love.

Three teams competed in 2024.  This season the PSSBL established a fourth team and a total of 62 players are participating in the 65+ summer schedule.  The average age for Midweek Daytime Baseball is 73 & 1/2.
