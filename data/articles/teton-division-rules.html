1 General

General PSSBL rules apply in all cases to Teton Division teams and players unless specific exceptions are noted for the Teton Division. These rules may be revised at any time by a majority vote of the General Managers (GMs)

1.1 Voting Rules and Definitions

A majority vote of GMs is a simple majority. A unanimous vote requires no dissenting votes (there may be abstentions) for passage. A near-unanimous vote can have no more than one dissenting vote. In matters requiring a vote of GMs, the commissioner will vote only to break a tie vote. The commissioner and any GM may introduce resolutions to be voted on.

2. Roster Rules

2.1 The Draft

Tryout players may be added to rosters through the annual draft. The selection order in the draft will be determined by the reverse order of win percentage for the previous regular season plus all playoff games. Tie games will count as half-win, half-loss in calculating these percentages. In the event that two or more teams have identical percentages, ties will be broken and the team selecting first will be determined by applying the following criteria, in the order specified: worst record in head-to-head games; most tie games; worst final finish in playoffs. If ambiguity persists, the commissioner will resolve the tie breaker with majority approval of the GMs. New teams will participate in the draft. Their position in the draft order will be based on perceived relative strength, as determined by the commissioner, subject to majority approval of the GMs.

2.2 Free Agents

Free agents are otherwise eligible players who can be added to rosters without going through the draft process. Each season, the GMs will determine the number (if any) of free agents that each team may add. Teams with lesser records (using the same criteria as draft order) may be granted more free agent picks than teams with better records. The allocation policy each season must be approved by a majority vote of the GMs. Players who were previously active members of a Teton roster are eligible to return to that team without counting as a free agent selection if they have been away from the team for no more than one intervening season. Players who were registered as inactive for the previous season are always eligible to return to active status and are not counted as free agents.

2.2-A Family Member Rule

That a immediate family member meaning, spouse, father, mother, son/brother or daughter/sister may be added to a team roster with no implication to teams draft or buddy pick status. Individual must be age eligible for the Teton Division.

2.3 Player Cuts

Teton division allows players to be cut in accordance with the general rules of the PSSBL. It is strongly urged that this process be utilized only with regard to maintaining team harmony and not for the purpose of obtaining competitive advantage.

2.4 Released Players

Any player may request their release from their current Teton team. Once released, the player is then eligible to enter the following draft, to be selected by any other team. A released player is not eligible to be a free agent selection for another Teton team within 12 months of his/her release.

2.5 Trades

Players may be traded between teams only with the approval of the player, all directly involved GMs, and the commissioner. The commissioner shall be empowered to introduce additional conditions or compensations if necessary to ensure a transaction fair to all parties and the rest of the division. Compensation for trades may include other players (with their approval) or draft picks. However the following restrictions apply:

2.5.1 Free agent selections may not be traded under any circumstances.

2.5.2 Traded draft picks can be no later than third round picks. Draft picks for future years may not be traded.

2.5.3 Any material goods offered as part of the terms of a trade must be for the benefit of the team.

2.6 Player Transfers

In rare circumstances, a released player may be granted permission to join a specific team without going through the draft process. Consideration will be given to the circumstances and the resulting impact to division parity. Terms of the transfer may include special forfeitures or compensations recommended by the commissioner. The transfer must be approved by near-unanimous vote of the GMs.

2.7 Supplementary Draft and Free Agent Picks

In addition to the normal draft and free agent picks, there may occasionally be cause to grant additional draft or free agent picks to compensate a team with unusual circumstances that significantly impact their ability to compete. The number and timing of such picks shall be determined by the commissioner, subject to near-unanimous approval of the GMs.

2.8 Rule Exceptions for Women and Women’s Teams

The Teton Division currently includes a women’s team (the Seattle Diamonds). The following roster rules apply to women players in general, to the Seattle Diamonds in particular, and to any potential future women’s team in the Teton division.

2.8.1 Any woman is eligible to play in the Teton division on any team, without restriction to age.

2.8.2 A women’s team may add female free agent players without restriction.

2.8.3 Men are eligible to play on a women’s team, but a women’s team must have at least as many active female players on their roster at any time as male players. Male players must meet all other eligibility requirements to play in the Teton division.

2.8.4 Women’s teams will participate in the annual draft with their draft position determined by the same criteria as other Teton teams.

2.8.5 If a male or female draft-eligible player has expressed a preference to be selected by a women’s team and a women’s team has committed to select that player in the draft, no non-women’s teams may select that player in the draft.

2.8.6. A women’s team may not draft a male player unless said player has specified prior preference or agreement to play on a women’s team.

2.8.7 A draft eligible female player may, with her prior permission, be eligible to be drafted by any team.

3. Minimum Playing Time

Minimum playing time is offered to all Teton Division players. It is not the intention to guarantee that all players receive ‘equal’ playing time. Participation is voluntary; while players are encouraged to participate, they will not be forced to play. Any decision to forego minimum participation requirements, either offensively or defensively, must be the choice of the player and not the manager. The opposing team manager should be notified prior to game time of any player(s) who have opted not to fully participate. Players are strongly discouraged from leaving the offensive or defensive lineup for ‘competitive’ reasons, and a GM may not suggest or deny a player their minimum playing time for competitive reasons. Leaving play for ‘competitive’ reasons may result in forfeiture of the game and a one game suspension to the player and/or GM for the first offense as determined by the Teton Division Commissioner. Additional penalties may be imposed as determined by the Teton Division Commissioner for additional offenses. Any player who feels their manager has defaulted on their responsibility to provide minimum playing time has the right to discuss the issue with their General Manager, the League Player Agent, and/or the Teton Division Commissioner.

3.1. Defense

3.1.1. Each player in the Teton Division will be offered a minimum of three innings of defense per nine inning game, with at least two of those innings scheduled for the first seven innings. No minimum is guaranteed if the player arrives after the first pitch of the game, or if a game is shortened (to less than seven innings) by unforeseen circumstances such as inclement weather or darkness.

3.1.2. Defensive position assignments are entirely at the discretion of the General Manager (or a designated Field Manager if applicable).

3.2. Offense

3.2.1. All players in attendance will be included each time through the batting order. Players arriving after the batting order has been exchanged with the opposing team must be added to the end of the batting order. A/B batting lineups are not permitted in Teton division.

3.2.2. A player may leave the offensive line-up without penalty (to the team) if the player is injured or is forced to leave the game for another commitment. The departing player’s turn at bat will be skipped without recording an out, with the provision that this does not apply where the departing player has been ejected by the umpire. In the case of an ejection, an out will be recorded the first time through the order at the ejected players turn in the order

3.2.3. If a player leaves the offensive lineup for any reason, that player is no longer eligible to return to the batting order.

4. Inning Limitation for Pitchers

4.1. Seven Inning Maximum

4.1.1. All pitchers in Teton Division games are limited to a maximum of seven innings per game. All innings pitched by an individual pitcher must be consecutive, regardless of the total number of innings pitched.

4.1.2. Partial innings pitched for the purposes of this rule are counted toward the seven inning maximum as full innings. For example, a pitcher entering the game with two outs in the first inning (and remaining in the game as the pitcher) has exhausted his/her eligibility after the seventh inning of that game.

4.1.3. Any pitcher hitting 4 batters shall be removed from pitching for safety reason and will not be allowed to reinter that position for the remainder of the game.

5. Courtesy Runners

5.1. Declared

A maximum of two players requesting courtesy runners can be declared prior to the exchange of line-up cards before a game. Courtesy runners are allowed to steal and have no other limitations.

5.2. Injuries

Injury replacements are allowed in addition to any declared courtesy runners. However, injury replacements are not allowed to steal bases. They may advance on a wild pitch, a passed ball, an errant pickoff attempt, or if the pitcher delivers from a full windup.

5.3 Replacement Runners

Substitute runners (courtesy or injury) must be the last batted out (unless that player is injured or was also declared as a player requesting a courtesy runner). In the event that no outs have been recorded in the game for the batting team, the courtesy runner must be the last player in the batting order. It is the joint responsibility of the scorekeeper and the batter requesting a substitute runner to identify and notify the replacement runner of his/her responsibilities. This should occur before the batter takes position in the batter’s box. The replacement runner should be wearing or holding a helmet and be prepared to immediately enter the game once time is called.

6. Wood Bat Requirement

Use of league-approved wood or wood composite bats is required for all Teton Division games. Bats are further restricted in Teton Division to be of one-piece construction, without a separate material making up the handle.

7. Miscellaneous

7.1 Home Team Dugout

7.1.1 The home team will occupy the first base dugout for each Teton Division game.

7.2 Taxi-Pool Players

7.2.1 PSSBL-wide Taxi-Pool rules are in effect for Teton Division rules and all Taxi-Pool players must meet general eligibility requirements to play in the Teton Division.

7.2.2 Taxi Pool players may not pitch unless agreed to by the opposing GM prior to the game.

7.3. Playoff Information

7.3.1. Playoff Seeding: Playoff seeds are determined by order of regular season finish and are not affected by playoff game results. The home team in each playoff game will be the higher seeded team, with the exception that in the (first) championship game, the team with no losses will be the home team.

7.3.2. Tie-breakers: For teams with identical regular-season records the tie-breaker will be determined based on the results of head-to-head competition. If ambiguity persists, the commissioner will resolve the tie-breaker with majority approval of the GMs.

7.4. General Meeting Attendance

7.4.1. A team that is not represented at two PSSBL General Meetings in any calendar year may be required to replace the General Manager following the second missed meeting, and prior to the next General Meeting of that calendar year.

7.4.2. Additional League penalties apply for any team not represented at a League General Meeting.

7.5. Forfeits

7.5.1. A forfeit by any Teton Division team during the season may result in the mandatory assignment of Taxi-Pool players to the roster for all remaining games on the schedule, arranged by and at the discretion of the Teton Division Commissioner.

7.5.2. A second forfeit during the season by any Teton Division team may result in exclusion from the playoffs and mandatory replacement of the General Manager prior to the next General Meeting of that calendar year.

7.5.3. Additional League penalties apply for any team forced to forfeit a regular season or playoff game.
