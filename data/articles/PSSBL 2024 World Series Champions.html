A record 23 PSSBL-based teams participated in national post-season play in 2024 either in Arizona, Florida or both. Many teams competed in the playoffs following round robin play. Four Puget Sound Senior Baseball League teams made it to the championship game in their respective division.

Ultimately two teams came home with the hardware. The 60+ Seattle Pilots, claimed the MSBL 60+ Desert Division World Series title.  The Pilots defeated the Vegas 60's at the Tempe Diablo Complex, spring training home of the Los Angeles Angels, 11-5. Read the whole story HERE.

                                          60+ Desert Champions - Seattle Pilots

The Northwest Tribe won the 45 & Over Wood Division at the NABA World Series.  The Tribe defeated the Wisconsin Rangers 16-5 at Goodyear Stadium, spring training home of the Cleveland Guardians & Cincinnati Reds. Justin Sherwin went 4 for 5 as the Tribe banged out a total of 24 hits.  Jason Rivers and Alan Webb also had four hits apiece.  Starting pitcher Chris Mills picked up the "W", scattering nine hits and allowing just two runs over six innings.  Fernando DaSilva closed out the game in relief. 

        

                                       45+ National Champions - Northwest Tribe

In addition, several PSSBL players won World Series rings with teams from outside the PNW.  Congrats to all our champions - way to represent the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League!
