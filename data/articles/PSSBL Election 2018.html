The annual PSSBL election will be held at the all-players league meeting tonight, Monday, December 4, 2017. Six 2018 Executive Board of Directors positions are voted on by the general membership. Board members serve one-year terms.

The elective positions are:  President, Vice-President, Secretary, Treasurer, Player Agent and Fields & Scheduling Director.

These six executive members join the commissioners of each division to sit on the PSSBL Board of Directors. The Division Commissioners are not selected in the General Election, but are voted for during the divisional breakout sessions immediately following the general session.

According to the Bylaws, any player in good standing — that is, any player who paid dues for the 2017 season in any way and is currently not subject to PSSBL disciplinary action or has an outstanding obligation — is welcome at tonight’s meeting and may vote. Please note proxies may not be voted. The meeting takes place at 7pm at the North Bellevue Community Center, 4063 148th Avenue NE, Bellevue, WA 98007, 425-452-7681. Several PSSBL elections in recent years have been decided by a single vote; why not make it yours?
