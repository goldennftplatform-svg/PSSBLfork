Welcome to the 36th season of the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League!  Online registration for the 2025 PSSBL baseball season is now open.

If you are a Returning Player, log in to your account first, click on your name to get the dropdown menu, select “Registration” and follow the prompts. "Buddy Picks" should respond to a registration email sent directly to you by your GM.

As you proceed through the online registration process, make sure to read the 2025 PSSBL Refund Policy and the PSSBL Waiver which you sign electronically.  If you encounter technical issues as you register, contact support@pssbl.com.

Thanks to Web Developer Rich Bonny, Michael Willard, Jake Liddil and Josh Emery for preparing our online registration.  Have a great 2025 season - Play ball!
