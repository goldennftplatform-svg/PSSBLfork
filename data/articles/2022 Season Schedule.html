The 2022 PSSBL regular season schedule is published.  As usual it takes a monumental effort to put together a 68-team, 10 division, 674-game regular season schedule.  Factors such as field quality, available days/times, spreading out a team’s schedule over four months, umpire group assignments, daylight/sunset issues, requested blackout dates, avoiding back-to-back-to-back dates, and other conflicts must be considered.

The PSSBL regular season gets underway the weekend of April 30-May 1.  To view your schedule, simply click on your team page at https://pssbl.com. 

Special thanks to Fields & Scheduling Director Joe Townsend for doing a great job of putting together the 2022 schedule and to Web Developer Rich Bonny and his team, Michael Willard and Derrick Hwang, for their continuing efforts at making our web site and scheduling software the best anywhere.  Play Ball!
