NOTE:  The COVID-19 Guidelines listed below were for the 2021 season.  The PSSBL is awaiting word from the State of Washington and our Field Providers as to what the guidelines will be for 2022.  We will publish those guidelines here as soon as we receive them from the various governing bodies.  Please check back for further updates.

 

  • Liability waivers noting the risks of participation will include virus & bacteria language as well as “hold harmless” provisions covering league, field owner & employees.  PSSBL Waiver MUST be signed before participating in tryouts and/or playing their first game by every player, coach, or other PSSBL personnel.
  • Players will not come to games if sick, sneezing, coughing or running a temperature.  Players will not come to a game if they have been exposed to a person with COVID-19 within the past 14 days.
  • Adults 65 years and older and people of any age who have serious underlying medical conditions are strongly encouraged to avoid large groups.
  • Players are allowed to remove facial coverings for scheduled games.  However, players are required to wear masks at all other times according to state and local guidelines.  NEW as of June 25, 2021:   Fully vaccinated individuals are exempt from the requirement to wear a face covering and are also exempt from physical distancing requirements.  Individuals do not need to supply any proof of vaccination to Seattle Parks.
  • Dugouts and area surrounding dugouts: 6-foot social distancing to be maintained - see above item pertaining to fully-vaccinated individuals.
  • Social distancing must be observed during the pre-game plate meeting between managers and umpires.  When holding a meeting on the mound all participants need to maintain safe distance from each other. The meeting is limited to a maximum of 3 people.
  • Umpires may handle game balls, although it may be determined by the officials that game balls will be provided by the team that is on defense.
  • No Sharing of batting helmets, catcher’s gear, towels, team coolers, water jugs, shared drinking stations, and similar items.  Initial supply of disinfectant and hand sanitizer will be provided by PSSBL.  After initial supply runs out, teams are expected to supply disinfectant and hand sanitizer for each game.
  • If a PSSBL participant tests positive for COVID during the season, the team manager MUST immediately report this to the league office.  Proper privacy regulations will be enforced.  That individual player would need to quarantine himself according to local guidelines and would be ineligible to participate for 14 days.  The player’s team may be subject to rescheduling or cancellation of games as determined by the League Office.
  • No post game handshakes or high fives – line up on baseline in front of dugout to salute your opponent in an appropriate manner like a tip of the hat or a “good game - congrats”.
  • Team Managers are responsible for leaving a clean dugout after each game – the same as during non-pandemic times.
  • If a league official or field monitor sees an infraction to the above stated rules, a warning will be issued.  A second violation will mean ejection from the game and possible forfeiture of the game.

In addition to the General PSSBL 2021 COVID-19 Guidelines, several field providers have asked for amendments regarding added conditions and practices.  Those additions to the original document are listed below.

BELLEVUE COLLEGE

  • Players will not come to games if sick, sneezing, coughing or running a temperature of 100.4° Players will self-check their temperature before heading to the ballpark.
  • The PSSBL follows the Healthy Washington Roadmap to Recovery plan.
  • Complete attendance records will be kept in scorebooks for a minimum of 28 days.
  • Teams will clean and disinfect high touch surfaces.
  • Defensive team will sanitize each ball to be put in play including foul balls.

CITY OF AUBURN (Brannan Park)

  • Players will self-check their temperature prior to arriving at the field.
  • Teams are expected to supply disinfectant and hand sanitizer for each game.  Clean dugout benches before and after games.  Use of on-site facilities for handwashing is encouraged.
  • If a participant develops symptoms during a game, they will be sent home.
  • Team managers are responsible for using disinfectant on all surfaces around the dugout, gates, and other areas of the field with which an infected individual may have had contact.
  • If a PSSBL participant tests positive for COVID during the season, in addition to the language stated above in the General Guidelines, the player's team and opposing team will be notified.

SEATTLE PARKS & SEATTLE SCHOOL DISTRICT (Lower Woodland, Magnuson Park, Chief Sealth High School, Rainier Beach High School, Whitman Middle School)

  • Participants should limit exposure to those outside the household unit during travel.  Only those in the same household should travel together, and if not in the same household, travel in separate vehicles if possible.
  • Players and coaches may not enter the playfield until the start of the reservation, and no equipment can be brought onto the field until the start of the reservation.  Waiting on the sidelines prior to the start of the reservation is not allowed.  All players and referees must vacate the field within 15 minutes of the end of the reservation.
  • All participants including players, coaches, umpires, other personnel involved in the game, and fans are required to wear masks and social distance, although players and officials may skip masking on the field of play.  Fully vaccinated individuals are exempt from the requirement to wear a face covering and are also exempt from physical distancing requirements.  Individuals do not need to supply any proof of vaccination to Seattle Parks.
  • Total attendance including players, coaches, umpires, other personnel and fans is limited to a maximum of 75 for each game.  All spectators of different households are to remain physically distant, 6 feet or more, as much as possible.
  • Use disinfectant and hand sanitizer often.  Clean high touch surfaces and disinfect as needed.
  • Managers will keep a roster of every athlete, staff and volunteer present at each practice, training session, and contest to assist with contact tracing in the event of a possible exposure.  Attendance records will be retained for a minimum of 28 days.
  • All equipment and garbage must be removed from the field within 15 minutes of the end of the reservation.

As of May 20, 2021, the following field providers are using Phase 2 guidelines, so facial coverings are required by all participants at all times everywhere at the field complex:  Chief Sealth High School, Heidelberg, Heritage Park, Mountlake Terrace High School, Lynnwood High School, Rainier Beach High School.

 

All other fields are following Phase 3 standards which means only players on the field in a game may skip wearing facial coverings.  However all participants must wear facial coverings at all other times and in all other areas of the complex including - but not limited to - dugouts, stands, restrooms, parking lots, etc.

 

These "Safe Play" guidelines, approved by the PSSBL Board of Directors on January 25, 2021, updated on May 20, 2021, and updated yet again on  June 25, 2021 are subject to change based on evolving conditions and direction from government agencies and officials at all levels.
