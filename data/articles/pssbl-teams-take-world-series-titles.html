Congratulations to two PSSBL-based teams who won World Series Championships this fall. The 3N2 Rangers dominated the L.A. Athletics 14-1 in the final to claim the 55 & Over Central Division MSBL title in Phoenix. Also in Phoenix, the Puget Sound Twins compiled an 8-1 record for the week in the 35 & Over MSBL Cactus Division. The Twins scored World Series rings with a 14-3 victory over Murietta (CA) Mutiny in the championship game.

Two other PSSBL-based teams made it all the way to the title game, but fell a bit short and wound up as runners-up. The Puget Sound Mariners lost to the Inland Angels to finish 2nd in the MSBL 60+ Cactus Division in Phoenix. The Washington Titans Vintage Division team (65 & Over) just missed out on a 2016 championship in Florida, losing out to a strong Terre Haute Volkers team in the final game of the Roy Hobbs World Series.
