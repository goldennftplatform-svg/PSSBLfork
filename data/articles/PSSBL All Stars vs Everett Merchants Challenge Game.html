The Everett Merchants of the Pacific International League have challenged the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League to a game.  The contest is set for TONIGHT, Saturday, June 1st at Funko Field in Everett.  First pitch is scheduled for 7:00pm.

 

From 2008 to 2014 PSSBL All Stars played a total of six charity games against the Merchants.  The PSSBL holds a 4-2 edge in that series and a 7-3 record overall against Pacific International League teams.

 

From 2018 to 2022 our PSSBL All Stars have a three game to one edge over the Highline Bears/DubSea Fish Sticks of the PIL. 

 

Founded in 1992, the Pacific International League is considered one of the premier Summer Collegiate Baseball organizations in the country.  In addition to the Merchants and Fish Sticks, other teams in the PIL include the Seattle Studs, Seattle Blackfins, Redmond Dudes, Northwest Honkers, and Gumberoos.

 

PSSBL Rocky Division Commissioner Brandon Williams will manage the PSSBL All Stars with assistant coaches Casey Bloom, Josh Jacobsen and Marty Bigelow.  For more information check out the Merchants website at https://everettmerchantsbaseball.com/

 

Admission is FREE! Let's have a big show of fan support for our PSSBL players and coaches TONIGHT in Everett.  Go All Stars!
