While many teams from the PSSBL are competing in various divisions at the 2023 MSBL World Series in Arizona, one pair of players made history.  Our very own Dick "Fitz" Fitzgerald and his longtime battery-mate Ken Combs were featured in the November 2nd edition of the MSBL World Series newsletter for an amazing accomplishment.

       Ken Combs (Left), Dick Fitzgerald (Right)

Fitzgerald, who now resides in Arizona, served in various capacities in the PSSBL including President, while Combs managed many successful teams. 

 Fitzgerald warms up with Combs for the 75 & Over USA Volkers

The pair once combined as the oldest battery to ever throw a pitch at Safeco Field (now T-Mobile Park) when Fitz tossed a ceremonial first pitch to Kenny at a Seattle Mariners game.  These two legends, Dick Fitzgerald and Ken Combs, are in the PSSBL Hall of Fame.
