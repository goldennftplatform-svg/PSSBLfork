The 2026 PSSBL Draft was held Saturday, March 28th, at Elliott Bay Brewing in Seattle. Thanks to all the tryout players who are now members of the non-profit PSSBL and welcome! We hope you have a fun summer in the league with your new team. Thanks to all the volunteers who helped run the tryouts, to all the GM's and other team representatives, and to the Board members for their tireless efforts. Thanks also to Elliott Bay Brewing for their hospitality during our draft day event.

While efforts were made to place tryout players on a PSSBL team or in a desired division, that was not always possible.  However, there may still be opportunities for undrafted players to get in some baseball this summer.  If you weren't selected, watch for information from Player Agent Marty Bigelow regarding our Waiver Wire and Taxi Pool. Or contact Marty Bigelow directly by clicking here.  Otherwise, refunds will be forthcoming to the players who were not drafted, and partial refunds will be credited to players who paid for a longer season than the one for which they were actually drafted.

With 74 teams across 10 divisions and 1,200+ players, the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League continues to be the premiere adult sports league - not only in the Puget Sound region, but the biggest and best in the U.S.!
