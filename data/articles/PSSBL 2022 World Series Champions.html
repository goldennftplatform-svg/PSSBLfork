While 18 PSSBL-based teams participated in national post-season play in 2022, sadly three teams planning to compete did not travel due to the devastation of Hurricane Ian in Florida.  Many of our members gave of their time and money to aid those affected by the natural disaster in Ft. Myers.  We thank them. 

Ultimately one team came home with the hardware. Congratulations to the 65+ Puget Sound Mariners, who claimed a second back-to-back national championship.

                                                   65+ Central Champions - Puget Sound Mariners

Here is a link to the full story of the 65+ Mariners exploits: https://msblnational.com/2022-65-central/

A number of PSSBL-based teams made it to their respective playoffs, including making it to the semi-finals. Congrats to all national World Series participants.  You've done the PSSBL proud!
