The annual PSSBL election will be held during an all-players league meeting TONIGHT, Tuesday, December 5, 2023. Five 2024 Executive Board of Directors positions are voted on by the general membership. Board members serve one-year terms.

In an effort to streamline decision making and reduce costs, the Board of Directors recently voted unanimously to amend the League Bylaws and combine the VP position with Secretary duties.

The elective positions for 2024 are:  President, Vice-President/Secretary, Treasurer, Player Agent and Fields & Scheduling Director.

These five executive members join the commissioners of each division to sit on the PSSBL Board of Directors. The Division Commissioners are not selected in the General Election, but are voted for during the divisional breakout sessions immediately following the general session.

To nominate yourself or another PSSBL member for a position, please reach out to a member of the 2024 Nominating Committee: Toraj Nowkhasteh, Steve Konek or Ryan Patrick.  Contact information for the committee members was sent via league-wide emails on October 10th and again on November 1st.    

According to PSSBL Bylaws, any player in good standing — that is, any player who paid dues for the 2023 season in any way and is currently not subject to PSSBL disciplinary action or has an outstanding obligation — is welcome at the 12/5/23 meeting and may vote. Please note proxies may not be voted.  The meeting takes place TONIGHT 12/5/23, 6:30pm in person at North Bellevue Community Center, 4063 148th Ave NE, Bellevue, WA 98007.  Several PSSBL elections in past years have been decided by a single vote; why not make it yours?
