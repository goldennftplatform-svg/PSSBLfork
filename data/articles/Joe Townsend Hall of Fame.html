Joe Townsend, Fields & Scheduling Director for the PSSBL, has been named to the Men's Senior Baseball League National Hall of Fame!  The announcement was made by MSBL President Steve Sigler, and a ceremony will take place during the MSBL World Series in Phoenix later this fall.

Joe joined the PSSBL in 2002, but his season was interrupted when he was called to duty in Bosnia from August of '02 to September, 2003.  Overall, Joe served four years in the U.S. Army and six years in the National Guard.

      JOE TOWNSEND

Returning to baseball in 2004, Joe chose to get more involved and has served on the PSSBL Board of Directors since 2007.  During his 15 years on the Board, one of the longest tenures of any board member in league history, Joe has been a key contributor in maintaining the PSSBL's dominance as one of the best-run adult sports leagues in the country.

Joe Townsend was inducted into the PSSBL Hall of Fame in 2016.  In addition to his stellar career stats as a hitter and on the mound, Joe has GM'd a number of teams and played in numerous MSBL World Series in Arizona where he has amassed five championship rings.

Beyond baseball, Joe works in law enforcement with juveniles, does lots of community outreach with young people, and is Head Baseball Coach at Thomas Jefferson High School.  He coached several years in Major League Baseball's RBI program, and even coaches Thomas Jefferson's girls bowling team, advancing to state and national tournaments.  For his tireless work with youth, Joe was awarded the MLK Distinguished Medal of Service Award several years ago.
