The Puget Sound Senior Baseball League held our second-ever social media video contest in 2025.  Many teams and individuals took up the challenge of submitting PSSBL-related videos which were featured on Instagram.  Contest rules restricted entries to active league members who followed our Instagram account and utilized the official hashtags #PSSBLBASEBALL2025 and #PSSBLBASEBALL.

Besides rewarding our members for showing off the prowess in hitting, catching, throwing, running bases and creativity, the PSSBL sought to increase the number of followers and activity on our IG account.  Engagement rose 154% since last December thanks in large part to the contest and everyone's participation.

After the competition closed September 30th, a panel of judges selected from the PSSBL Board of Directors reviewed the entries and determined the winners.

FIRST PLACE - Sierra Saints for "We Came to Play" Reel.  Prize: One Paid 2026 Member Dues

SECOND PLACE - Denali Giants for "Bobby Bond Walks It Off".  Prize: $100 Credit at the PSSBL Online Store.  View the Giants Video HERE

THIRD PLACE - Rocky Ravens for "Strike Three Reel".  Prize: One Dozen Official PSSBL Baseballs.  View the Ravens video HERE

Congratulations to the winners and runners-up. Thanks to everyone who participated.  We plan to sponsor another video contest in 2026.  Make sure to follow us on Instagram, X, Facebook and our PSSBL YouTube channel.
