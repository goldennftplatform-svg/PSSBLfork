The annual PSSBL election will be held during an online all-players league meeting Monday night, December 6, 2021. Six 2022 Executive Board of Directors positions are voted on by the general membership. Board members serve one-year terms.

The elective positions are:  President, Vice-President, Secretary, Treasurer, Player Agent and Fields & Scheduling Director.

These six executive members join the commissioners of each division to sit on the PSSBL Board of Directors. The Division Commissioners are not selected in the General Election, but are voted for during the divisional breakout sessions immediately following the general session.

According to PSSBL Bylaws, any player in good standing — that is, any player who paid dues for the 2021 season in any way and is currently not subject to PSSBL disciplinary action or has an outstanding obligation — is welcome at Monday’s 12/6/21 meeting and may vote. Please note proxies may not be voted. The meeting takes place at 7pm online at Zoom.com.  If you are an eligible PSSBL member, check the email you use to log into your account on the web site.  You'll see a link and passcode to join the Zoom session.  Several PSSBL elections in recent years have been decided by a single vote; why not make it yours?
