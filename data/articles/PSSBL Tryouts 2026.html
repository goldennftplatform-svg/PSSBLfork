FINAL 2026 TRYOUTS SESSION - TODAY!!!  SUNDAY, MARCH 22nd

Interlake High School, 16245 NE 24th St, Bellevue, WA 98008

 

9:00 AM - Noon = Tryout Players Age 40 & Over

Noon - 3:00 PM = Tryout Players Age 18-39

 

Tryouts are FREE to attend.  It's a turf field, so no metal cleats, please.

 

The non-profit Puget Sound Senior Baseball League (PSSBL), the #1 largest amateur adult baseball league in the U.S., is holding open tryouts for all baseball players, age 18 to 65+, in preparation for the 2026 season.

Players must be at least 18 years of age to participate in any official PSSBL event. Online registration is now open.

A league-wide draft will be held Saturday, March 28th.  All tryout players who wish to be drafted must pay membership dues by Friday, March 27th.

 

The PSSBL is comprised of 1,200 players forming 74 teams in ten different divisions. The divisions are organized based on age and skill level, ranging from advanced to recreational. Formed in 1989, this year marks the PSSBL's 37th season.

 

For more information contact PSSBL Player Agent Marty Bigelow .
