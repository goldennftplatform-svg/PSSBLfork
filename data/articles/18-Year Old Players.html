The PSSBL Board of Directors recently voted to allow 18-year old players to join the League and play in 2020.  Previously, only players who were age 19 and over were accepted as members of our non-profit organization.

There are certain conditions that 18-year old ballplayers must adhere to.

  • Players are allowed at tryouts ONLY if they have already turned 18 at the time of the tryout.
  • Players must sign their own waiver form as an adult and may be required to show proof of age.
  • Players competing on their High School team may not participate until after their HS season concludes.
  • Players are NOT allowed on the field, bullpens or in the dugouts at any PSSBL game until they have turned 18.
  • Players who turn 18 after tryouts have concluded, can register via the Free Agent tool and will be eligible to join a team once they are 18.

  • Players are eligible to be added as buddy picks in age-eligible divisions once they have turned 18.
