With a dramatic finish in the bottom of the ninth inning, the PSSBL All Stars edged the Everett Merchants 7-6, Tuesday night, July 15th.  The well-attended contest took place at Funko Field in Everett, home of the Aqua Sox.   This was the eighth time since 2008 the Merchants of the Pacific International League challenged the PSSBL to a fundraising baseball game. The PIL is one of the premier summer collegiate leagues in the country.  The All Stars now own a 5-3 series edge over the Merchants. The PSSBL played the Highline Bears/DubSea Fish Sticks four times from 2018-2022.  Overall the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League holds a 8-4 series lead over PIL teams. 

 

Josh Richardson of the Sierra Division Rage started on the mound for the PSSBL and hurled two scoreless innings. The PSSBL jumped out to a 1-0 lead in the third inning, and tacked on three more in the top of the fourth.  The Merchants countered with a two run home run in the bottom of the fifth.  The All Stars extended their lead to 5-2 in the top of the sixth. However, the Merchants put up a 3-spot in the bottom of the frame to tie up the game at 5 apiece.  

 

The All Stars managed to push across two runs in the seventh inning to take a 7-5 lead.  The Merchants battled back in the bottom of the eighth.  With one run in for Everett and runners on first and second with no outs, PSSBL reliever Shungo Najima of the Cascade Division Nationals induced a double play and followed it with a rally-killing strikeout.

 

The tightly contested game moved into the ninth inning.  The visiting All Stars failed to extend their lead in the top of the frame.  In the bottom of the ninth, closer Dan Eck of the Sierra Division Ravens was called on to protect a slim 7-6 lead.  He walked the leadoff hitter, but then struck out the next Merchants batter.  Eck got the next man to hit a ground ball to second base, which turned into a 4-6-3 game-ending double play and an All Stars win.

 

Eight different PSSBL pitchers combined for 8 strikeouts, led by Josh Richardson, Lucas Kunkel, and Sam Ballweg with 2 K's each.  Ballweg got the "W" and Eck was credited with the Save.  Hitting stars include Joey Clancy who batted 1 for 2 with 2 Walks, 1 RBI and 1 Run, and Evan Carl, who batted 1 for 4 with 1 RBI and 1 Run. Other offensive contributors included Shane Kessel, Clint Maschoff, and Kyle Conwell.

 

Amari Goodfellow and Bryce Johnson provided the bulk of the offense for the Merchants with a combined 5 for 8, 5 RBI and 3 Runs scored.  Everett pitcher Trey Nance was tagged with the loss.

 

Rocky Division Commissioner Brandon Williams handled GM duties for the PSSBL All Stars assisted by coaches Casey Bloom and Josh Jacobsen.  The evening began with a heartfelt tribute and ceremonial first pitches honoring the late PSSBL & National MSBL Hall of Famer Joe Townsend, who passed away unexpectedly earlier this year.  Townsend managed the PSSBL All Stars to a 7-2 record in the first nine PSSBL-PIL challenge games.
