Midweek Daytime Baseball Returns for 2026

In 2024, the PSSBL introduced an experimental program to play midweek daytime baseball. We plan to continue this program for 2026. This special division is open to registered, active PSSBL members aged 65 and over. Many are players on existing PSSBL teams, although we will be opening participation this year to players who wish to play only in the daytime division, without requiring a special exemption.

We are planning to field four teams this season and the following dues structure will be applied:

$130 (Player on an existing PSSBL team)

$260 (Player only playing Midweek – includes Base Dues)

The deadline for registration & payment is May 13, 2026. To register, go to your registration page (Login to your PSSBL account and select the "Registration" menu item below your name in the top right corner of the page). If you have not yet registered for 2026, you will need to do that first. If you are eligible to participate (Age 65+), you will see a section on your registration page titled "2026 Daytime Baseball Registration". Click the "Register and Pay" button to continue the process. [Note: if you have paid no dues to this point in the season, you may be charged $300 since the $40 early payment deadline has passed. If so, you will receive a $40 manual credit at a later time].

Each of the four teams will play a 9-game schedule with games each week (no bye weeks), principally on Tuesdays or Wednesdays.  We’ll still use a single umpire per game. As we did last year, players will wear a favorite uniform.

Special rules will apply to this division. See the bottom of this page for these special adjustments to standard PSSBL rules.

Rule adjustments for Midweek Daytime Baseball

Below are the special rules adopted for the 2025 midweek daytime season. There may be some minor adjustments applied for 2026. This page will be updated with any such changes.

  • All players bat in their respective team's batting order unless declined by player.
  • Minimum of three innings on defense offered unless declined by player, preferably rotating after two innings in the field to try and provide more field time. Free substitution of players on defense.
  • Pitchers limited to a maximum of five innings per game. Pitcher reentry is acceptable.
  • No stealing unless the pitcher goes into a full windup. No hit-and-run. Runner(s) may advance one base at their own risk on Passed Ball or Wild Pitch (ball must leave the catcher's circle).
  • Base Runners may only take a lead as far as the cutout prior to the pitch being delivered. Once the pitch is delivered (leaves the pitcher's hand), runners may take a secondary lead.
  • Even though there is no stealing when the pitcher is in the stretch, runners are still susceptible to pick-off throws. A pickoff attempt by the pitcher must be a Tag Play. Catchers may throw to a base to pick off a runner as a Force Play -- no tag necessary. The runner(s) may advance one base on an overthrow -- at their own risk.
  • No Bunting with a runner on third base.
  • A batter or runner may not be forced out at first base or second base by an outfielder on a batted ball that hits the outfield ground. No exceptions for relay attempts.
  • Five runs scored max per inning. Does not apply to last inning.
    Wood bats will be used per PSSBL Rules.
  • Courtesy Runners Allowed -- No Limit -- Most recent batter not on base is the runner.
  • Time Limit: No new inning after 2 hours 45 Minutes from First Pitch.
  • Once a runner on third is halfway home, he is committed and cannot retreat to third base. Halfway is umpire judgment call. The play at the plate is a force play; no tag needed; no blocking plate permitted.
