While a record number of PSSBL-based teams participated in national post-season play, only one came home with the hardware.  Congratulations to the 65+ Puget Sound Mariners, who survived four playoff games in a span of 30 hours to claim a national championship.

Here is a link to the full story of the 65+ Mariners exploits: https://msblnational.com/2021-65-cactus/

A number of PSSBL-based teams made it to their respective playoffs, including making it to the semi-finals.  The 70+ Washington Titans and the 65+ Seattle Mariners silver-medaled, losing in their respective championship contests.

Congrats to all national World Series participants.  You've done the PSSBL proud!
