Due to very unfavorable conditions forecasted and the increased risk of injury, the 4th and final PSSBL Tryouts on Sunday, March 23rd have been canceled.  The tryouts were originally scheduled for the hybrid field (turf infield, clay mound, grass outfield) at Interlake High School in Bellevue.

The National Weather Service and other forecasting agencies predict rain chances of 80-100% starting late Saturday night and continuing all day Sunday.  As much as one-quarter to one-half inch of rain is expected.  Gusty winds at times are included in the forecast.

Players who have registered but did not attend any of our three earlier tryouts may still be eligible for the 2025 Draft.  Watch for an email with instructions on how to be draft-eligible, working with PSSBL Player Agent Marty Bigelow.

The 2025 PSSBL Draft takes place Saturday, March 29th.  All paid tryout players will be eligible to be drafted to a team.  Tryout players must pay full league dues by Friday, March 28, 2025 at 11:59:59 PM to be in the draft pool.  Unpaid players will not be considered.

Questions?  Contact PSSBL Player Agent Marty Bigelow .
