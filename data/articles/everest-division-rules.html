Last Updated 3/30/2013

1. Competitive Level

The Everest Division is a competitive division so there will be no minimum playing time rules

2. Running for Catcher

Teams will always have the option of running for the catcher with two outs - last batted out will run. No manager approval at home plate meeting needed.

3. Non-Runners

We will allow two non runners per team. They must be declared before the game and marked on the lineup card given to the opposing team. The last batted out will run for the non runner.

4. Catcher Possession

The Everest Division will play possession at home plate for the catcher.

5. No Age Exemption

No player under the PSSBL league age of 55 will be allowed except a variance has been granted by the Commissioner or vote of the GM’s. Once a variance is granted that player is legal until he reaches the age of 55.

6. Buddy Picks

The Everest Division is an open buddy pick division.

7. Trades

All trades must be approved by the Commissioner and Player Agent. No trade will be allowed that unduly encumbers a teams’ future viability.

8. Official Bat

Everest Division players will not be allowed to use a composite bat of any type. Players must use all wood bats.

9. Taxi Player Use

A taxi player must get at least three innings in the field and at least two at bats.
