The fourth and final tryout session for the 2017 season will be held today, Sunday, March 26th at Edmonds Community College.  Edmonds Community College is located at 20000 68th Avenue West in Lynnwood.  The tryouts will be held rain or shine.  Triton Field is an all-turf facility with an adjacent indoor building with batting cages and pitching tunnels.  Metal cleats are not allowed at EdCC. 

The Puget Sound Senior Baseball League (PSSBL), one of the largest amateur adult baseball leagues in the U.S., is conducting open tryouts for all baseball players, age 19 and over, the final weekend in March. Players age 35 & over should attend the 9:00am-12:00pm session (check in at 8:30am). Players age 19 & over should attend the 12:00pm-3pm session (check in at 11:30pm).

Those who turn 19 any time during the 2017 calendar year - even as late as December 31, 2017 - are eligible to join the league this season. There is no fee to try out for the PSSBL.

**If you are a Buddy Pick for a team in a division which allows such roster additions, please do not sign up for the tryouts & draft unless you are planning to play for a second team as a draft selection.** 

The non-profit PSSBL is comprised of over 1,000 players forming 64 teams in eight different divisions. The divisions are organized based on age and skill level, ranging from highly competitive to purely recreational. Formed in 1989, this year marks the PSSBL's 29th season of operation. See you at the tryouts!
