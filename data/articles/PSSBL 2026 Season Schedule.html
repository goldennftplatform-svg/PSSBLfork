The 2026 PSSBL regular season schedule is published.  As usual it takes a monumental effort to put together a 74-team, 10 division, 732-game regular season schedule.  Factors such as field quality, available days/times, spreading out a team’s schedule over four months, umpire group assignments, daylight/sunset issues, requested blackout dates, avoiding back-to-back-to-back dates, and other conflicts must be considered.

The PSSBL regular season gets underway Friday, May 1st.  To view your schedule, simply click on your team page at https://pssbl.com.
