Many PSSBL players either coach youth baseball or have children who play. Arm injuries in particular are common in young athletes, even more so since year around leagues became commonplace. Some youths participate on more than one team. Young athletes are encouraged to focus on a single sport and even a single position within a given sport at increasingly younger ages. In my clinic, I see many young players with arm problems. The types of injuries vary from soft tissue trauma to tendons and ligaments, to skeletal injuries in growing bones. Activities and behaviors have been identified that increase the risk of injury in young athletes. Fortunately, preventative measures exist that can be utilized to reduce this risk.
