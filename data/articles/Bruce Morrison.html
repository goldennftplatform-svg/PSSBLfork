Longtime player, GM and friend, Bruce Morrison, passed away August 30, 2021 after a courageous battle with cancer.

  

He had a 20+ year career in the PSSBL with the Green Jackets, Cyclones, Aqua Sox, and most recently, Crawfords and Osprey.  For his substantial contributions, Bruce was recognized with a PSSBL Career Achievement Award this past spring. 

 

Bruce also played in the MSBL World Series over 17 seasons in various age brackets.  In addition, he played in the MSBL World Series not only in Father-Son, but at one point in Father-Son & Grandson!

 

Bruce was also heavily involved in Seattle Mariners Fantasy Camps and managed multiple traveling teams to Palm Springs, Palm Desert, San Diego and Monterey.

 

Friends, team mates and opponents remember that Bruce was a one of a kind – on and off the baseball field, plus he was an amazing ambassador of baseball and the PSSBL.

 

Bruce Morrison will be sorely missed!  Details on any ceremony and/or remembrance are forthcoming.  Please keep Bruce's spouse, Becky, and his family in your prayers..
