In order to minimize the number of people onsite at any given time and to minimize contact among those in attendance, a special plan is being put in place for the 2021 PSSBL tryouts. 

Highlights of this plan:

 

  • Tryout players will attend tryouts in groups of ~8-12 players by appointment.
  • Sessions will last approximately an hour and a half.
  • Players will be grouped by age range and estimated skill level.  
  • Online registration is strongly encouraged - tryout players should register and sign the waiver online in order to be scheduled. If a player shows up unscheduled, we'll do our best to register them on-site.
  • Tryout players should view "An Introduction to the PSSBL" video on the website prior to attending a tryout.
  • Bib numbers are assigned in advance and players pick up bibs when they arrive.
  • Scouting can be done either in-person or remotely via streaming video.  Recorded video will be made available for additional viewing on the website.

 

Registration & Scheduling Details:

Tryout players must register online in advance of the tryouts in order to have their tryout appointment scheduled.  Instructions here: https://pssbl.com/#!/articles/faq-registration#SignUp

The League Player Agent will send a survey link to registered tryout players to collect input for scheduling appointments.  The goal will be to schedule groups of players to complement each other based on age range, skill / experience level and positions (i.e. - minimize position overlap).  

Based on the survey responses, the Player Agent will create the schedule, assign bib numbers, and notify the players of their tryout appointments as well as provide additional instructions for what to expect when they arrive.  Tryout appointments will also be noted in the draft notes field on the player’s tryout profile page.

Players are required to wear a facemask and maintain social distancing to the extent possible..
