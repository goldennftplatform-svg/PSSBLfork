When PSSBL member Rand Gilbertson passed away during the 2022-23 off-season, he left a hole - not only in the Adirondack Generals roster - but also in the hearts of his teammates and opposing players.  Gilbertson played for the Generals from 2015 to 2022, winning division titles in 2021 and 2022.  In 2023 a salute to Gilbertson marks Generals uniforms, #12 RG.  

                                          

                               Rand Gilbertson                                          #12 RG Honor Patch on Generals Jerseys

Respected by teammates and opponents alike, Adirondack Solar Sox GM Stuart Chernis states "Rand always showed great sportsmanship and was always willing to help other teams.  The Solar Sox became acquainted with him as he taxied for us multiple times over the years.  Rand is remembered most for always hitting .500 for us and being willing to catch multiple innings.  We enjoyed his German Shepherd companion as they were always first to arrive at the field." 

The Solar Sox honored the memory of Rand Gilbertson with a generous donation to the Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center.

                           

Solar Sox GM Stuart Chernis presents the Rand Gilbertson tribute to Generals GM Rich Bonny and Adirondack Commissioner Carl Gleason with players from both teams looking on.  The ceremony took place July 8, 2023 at Peter Kirk Park/Lee Johnson Field in Kirkland.
