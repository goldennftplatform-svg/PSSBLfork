The non-profit Puget Sound Senior Baseball League continues planning for an abbreviated 2020 baseball season.  In a year of change and challenges, we look forward to getting back on the field!

The tryouts are concluded.  Emails have been sent to all tryout players urging them to pay membership dues by the extended deadline Monday night, July 6th, at 6pm to be draft-eligible.  Instead of the usual in-person draft, 2020's draft will be held online via Zoom covering three days, July 6th, 7th and 8th.  All 10 divisions will be drafting.

Monday, July 6th = Rainier, Everest, Smoky, Sierra; Tuesday, July 7th = Cascade, Adams, Adirondack; Wednesday, July 8th = Teton, Denali, Rocky

Drafting begins at 7pm each evening.  GM's will receive an email with a Zoom meeting invitation and draft instructions.

As we await the transition to Phase 3 of the state's reopening plan in King, Pierce and Snohomish Counties, the Board of Directors has a plan in place to provide a quality baseball experience for our members.  We are targeting the weekend of August 1-2 as opening weekend with an 8-week season extending to September 27th.  Single game division championship contests would be scheduled October 1-4.  If Phase 3 is initiated a bit later than what is projected, we have plans to deal with that as well and still enjoy a quality season.

The PSSBL has established "Play Safe" rules so that every participant can stay healthy and safe.  These rules are required by our field providers and expected by our members.  We will constantly monitor the effectiveness of the "Play Safe" rules and moderate them as conditions change or as needed.

Most questions can be answered by viewing "About The PSSBL"; Further information may be obtained by contacting a volunteer PSSBL Staff Member.
