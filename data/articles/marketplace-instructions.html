2021 PSSBL.com Market Place Ads:

Technical Submission details:

 To submit your ad to the marketplace, you should provide 3 pieces of information:

 1)      Business card image file

2)      A link to your website

3)      Up to 5 keywords

 Further details:

 1) The business card image should be attached as a file in either .jpg or .png format. The preferred dimensions are 315 x 180 pixels.  Images submitted in other sizes will be rescaled to this size.

 2) When viewers click on your business card advertisement, they will be directed to your website. Please provide the complete URL of your website (e.g. - "http://www.axebat.com"). If you do not have an associated website, you can provide a link to something appropriate, for example, a listing of your business on Yelp, or the online Yellow Pages.

 3) You can provide up to 5 keywords to associate with your ad. Each keyword can actually consist of up to two words, if appropriate (for example, "Auto Repair" or "Tax Preparation"). Anyone viewing the marketplace page can filter the ads on the page to search for specific terms, so choose common keywords that viewers are likely to search for.
