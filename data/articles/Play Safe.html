PSSBL "PLAY SAFE" RULES FOR 2022

o             Home plate umpire will stand behind the pitcher’s mound with an appropriate distance between himself and the pitcher.  Umpires will wear face masks and gloves regardless of where they position themselves.

o             Dugouts and area surrounding the dugouts will be used observing social distancing requirements for players and equipment.

o             Temperatures of each participant must be taken prior to every game.  Any participant with a temperature of 100.4° or higher will be sent home.  Each team’s manager is responsible for keeping a record of temperature outcomes in the team scorebook or other format for each game.  Teams provide their own “no touch” thermometer.

o             No sharing of equipment including, but not limited to helmets, bats, gloves, bat weights, pine tar rags, catchers gear, towels and team water coolers or shared drinking stations.

o             Catcher and batter are required to wear surgical-type (or similar) masks.  If a division allows stealing, the first baseman must wear a mask.  Depending on specific field provider and/or local community rules, all participants may be required to wear masks.

o             When holding a meeting on the mound between catcher, pitcher and/or manager, all participants must stay a safe distance from each other.  The number of people at a mound meeting is limited to 3.  Social distancing must be observed during the pre-game plate meeting between managers and umpires.

o             No spitting or licking of fingers (pitchers, for example)

o             Baseballs are to be sanitized with disinfectant wipes after each batter sequence and after retrieving foul balls before being returned to the umpire.  The batting team is responsible for this safety measure.

o             No post game handshakes or high fives.

o             Every team provides disinfectant gear and hand sanitizer at each game.  Teams wipe down commonly-touched areas before the leave the field/dugout.

Approved by the PSSBL Board of Directors June 15, 2020
