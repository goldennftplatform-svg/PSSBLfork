The 2024 PSSBL regular season schedule is published.  As usual it takes a monumental effort to put together a 71-team, 10 division, 705-game regular season schedule.  Factors such as field quality, available days/times, spreading out a team’s schedule over four months, umpire group assignments, daylight/sunset issues, requested blackout dates, avoiding back-to-back-to-back dates, and other conflicts must be considered.

The PSSBL regular season gets underway the weekend of May 3 - May 5.  To view your schedule, simply click on your team page at https://pssbl.com. 

Special thanks to Fields & Scheduling Director Joe Townsend for doing a great job of putting together the 2024 schedule and to Web Developer Rich Bonny and Asst. Web Developer Michael Willard for their continuing efforts at making our web site and scheduling software the best anywhere.  Play Ball!
