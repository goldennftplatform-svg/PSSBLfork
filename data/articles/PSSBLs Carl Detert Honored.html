Veteran PSSBL player Carl Detert was recently named national Player of the Week by MABL/MSBL.  The Puget Sound Senior Baseball League, now in its 35th season, is a longtime affiliate of MSBL. 

The imposing righty pitcher recently achieved several statistical milestones.  Carl recorded his 2,500th strikeout last July and chalked up his 200th Career Win earlier this year.

Carl Detert, who was inducted into the PSSBL Hall of Fame in 2013, can also get it done at the plate.  His slash line is .335/.422/.446.  Carl's teams have won numerous division championships and several national World Series titles.  He was World Series MVP for the national champion 35+ Puget Sound Gators in 2007.
