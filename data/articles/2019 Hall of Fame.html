Now in our 31st season, the PSSBL has been the home to many outstanding ballplayers.  The PSSBL Hall of Fame was established in 2011 to honor those exhibiting exceptional performance on the field, in administrative duties, or both.  Each year the PSSBL Hall of Fame Committee receives a considerable number of worthy nominations from around the league, and this year was no exception.  The committee members have the very difficult task of narrowing the field to the final list of inductees. 

Here is the 2019 Class entering the PSSBL Hall of Fame:

Rich Bonny, Larry Davis, Paul Fisher, Bill Mann

Congratulations to each one of these deserving players!  In addition to being premier athletes, they are great ambassadors for the PSSBL and the game of baseball.  They join 48 players previously inducted into “The Hall”.

The 2019 Induction Ceremony will take place at T-Mobile Park tonight, Monday, June 3rd.  We'll have a BBQ and program at Edgar's Home Run Porch (left field area).  We get the HR Porch for the entire evening as the M's take on the Houston Astros.  Gates open at 5:10pm.  The actual presentation ceremony takes place from 5:35 - 6:10pm.  There are still a handful of tickets available.  If you still want to attend this great event, contact Tom Evans Krause right away.
