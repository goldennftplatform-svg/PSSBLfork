The PSSBL Hall of Fame, established in 2011, welcomes eight new inductees in 2022.  By virtue of their performance on the field over time or tireless work building the league as GM/administrator or a combination of both, these members are honored and thanked.

JEFF "GRANNY" AUSTIN, BRAD CLINE, KEN COMBS, TROY HANSON, BRETT LEE, BILL MAYOVSKY, CHET "JET" MORGAN, JEFF NARANJO

Congratulations to each of these new members of the prestigious PSSBL Hall of Fame!  The formal induction ceremony will take place Monday, June 13th at T-Mobile Park prior to the Mariners-Twins game.  Tickets will go on sale in coming days..
