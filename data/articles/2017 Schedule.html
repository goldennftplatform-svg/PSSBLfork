The 2017 PSSBL regular season schedule is published. As usual it takes a monumental effort to put together a 63-team, 8 division, 676-game regular season schedule. Factors such as field quality, available days/times, spreading out a team’s schedule over four months, umpire group assignments, daylight/sunset issues, requested blackout dates, avoiding back-to-back-to-back dates, and other conflicts must be considered.

Another factor this year is that a number of fields we normally reserve are unavailable this summer including Sammamish HS and Mountlake Terrace HS.  Russell Road will go offline later this summer for renovation.

The PSSBL regular season gets underway the weekend of April 29-30. To view your schedule, simply click on your team page at https://pssbl.com. Special thanks to Fields & Scheduling Director Joe Townsend for doing a great job of putting together the 2017 schedule and to Web Developer Rich Bonny for the new web site and scheduling software.
