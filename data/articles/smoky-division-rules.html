1. Forfeits

1.1. After the first forfeit, the Commissioner shall warn the team.

1.2. If that team forfeits a second time, the team shall be disbanded. If a team is disbanded, every effort will be made to readjust the playing schedule so the remaining teams do not lose any game dates because of the removal of the forfeiting team.

2. Courtesy Runners

2.1. Each team is allowed to designate two (2) players for whom courtesy runners will be used.

2.2. These players must be designated prior to the start of the game or their courtesy runner option is waived.

2.3. If a player for whom a courtesy runner was not designated needs a runner during the game, a pinch runner may be used.

2.4. The courtesy runner shall be the last batted out. A batted out is defined as a batter who, at the immediate conclusion of his at bat, has not reached base or has been put out and is no longer on base. That is, if the batter is put out before safely reaching base or is put out trying to stretch a single into a double or over running a base, he is a batted out. Once the ball is returned to the mound or time is called, and a batter remains on base, he can no longer be considered a batted out. If no player has been a batted out, then the courtesy runner would be the last batter in the order who is not designated to have a courtesy runner.

2.5. A batter must touch 1st base (or 2nd or 3rd base if there is an extra base hit) and "time" must be called before the courtesy runner replaces that player.

2.6. A courtesy runner does not have to be used in every single incident. A player, even though designated to have a courtesy runner, may run for himself, if he sees fit to do so.

2.7. If a player for whom no courtesy runner has been designated at the start of the game becomes injured to the extent that he cannot run (players may still be able to field or pitch, but not be able to run the bases), a pinch runner must be used.

2.8. With two outs, a courtesy runner will be allowed for the catcher at the option of the batting team.

3. Wood Bats

Only "all wood" bats and "composite-wood" bats (as defined in PSSBL rules) are allowed.

4. Dugouts

The home team shall use the 3rd base dugout and the visiting team shall use the 1st base dugout.

5. Player Rosters

5.1. Ex-Professional Players

An ex-professional player must be out of professional baseball for at least ten (10) years to be eligible to play in the Smoky Division.

5.2. Buddy Picks

The number of buddy picks shall be an unlimited number per team.

5.3. Draft Picks

The order of draft picks for the next season will also be finalized for each team immediately after the end of the preceding season. Draft picks shall be determined as a result of a team’s overall winning percentage (including the regular season and the playoffs i.e. wins/(wins + losses) with ties not included in calculation) and trades.

IMPORTANT: If a Smoky team makes a trade in good faith with another Smoky team which is subsequently penalized, it shall be assumed for trade purposes that the team not in violation will retain its pre-penalty position in the draft.

5.4. Taxi-Pool Players

5.4.1. A Taxi Pool player may be used if a team expects to have less than twelve (12) players at a game.

5.4.2. Taxi Pool players may be from within the Smoky Division and must be ages 45 and over.

5.4.3. The opposing team must be notified about the Taxi Pool player before game time at home plate when the line-ups are exchanged.

5.4.4. A Taxi Pool player must play at least three (3) innings in the field and get two (2) at-bats.

5.5. Playoff Eligibility

In addition to any league-wide eligibility requirements, a player must "participate" in 8 of his team's Smoky games to be "Smoky playoff eligible." Players will get "participation" credit for attending his Smoky team’s game even if he does not make have a plate appearance, play in the field or pinch run if his attendance is noted in the game statistics (box score) posted on "www.pssbl.com":http://www.pssbl.com/.

A player may request an injury waiver from the Smoky Commissioner for games missed due to injury. The general manager of the "injured" player must request in writing an injury waiver for the player before his team’s last game of the regular season. A list of the games and dates missed due to injury must be included in the waiver request. In his sole discretion, the Smoky Commissioner may request additional documentation to substantiate the injury.

The Smoky Commissioner will publish either a "draft" list of eligible and/or ineligible players by the end of Sunday of Labor Day (i.e. the Sunday of the week playoffs start) using the game statistics posted on www.pssbl.com. Smoky division general managers will have until 5:00 p.m. on Labor Day to request "eligibility" for players not on the draft eligibility list (e.g., to correct administrative errors made by the Smoky Commissioner when he drafted the list). Use of an "ineligible" player will result in a forfeit for his team if (1) the opposing team’s general manager notifies the Smoky Commissioner in writing of the alleged use of an "ineligible player" within 24 hours of the start of the applicable playoff game and (2) the Smoky Commissioner is able to confirm to his sole satisfaction that the player was (a) "ineligible" and (b) played in the playoff game in question.

The purpose of this rule is to prevent teams from gaining a competitive advantage during the playoffs by using players who "are not part of the team" (i.e. "sand bagging"). The Smoky Commissioner will determine playoff eligibility based on this rule. The Smoky Commissioner’s decisions on playoff eligibility are "final."

6. Minimum Playing Time

There is no minimum playing time rule except for the Taxi-Pool player requirements. Smoky players should be encouraged to speak first with his or her team GM or field manager before going to the PSSBL Player Agent about lack of playing time.
