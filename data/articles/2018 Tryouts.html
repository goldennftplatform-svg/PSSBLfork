The non-profit Puget Sound Senior Baseball League (PSSBL), one of the largest amateur adult baseball leagues in the U.S., is conducting open tryouts for all baseball players, age 19 to 65+.

Final Tryout Session (and the rain is clearing out just in time!):

  • TODAY - Saturday, March 24th at Russell Road Park                                            24400 Russell Road, Kent, WA 98032

Players age 35 & over should attend the 9:00am-12:00pm session (check in at 8:30am). Players age 19 & over should attend the 12:00pm-3pm session (check in at 11:30pm).  A league-wide draft will be held Saturday, March 31st.  Russell Road Park is all-turf, so no metal cleats, please.

Those who turn 19 any time during the 2018 calendar year - even as late as December 31, 2018 - are eligible to join the league this season. Tryouts are FREE - there is no charge to try out for the PSSBL.

The non-profit PSSBL is comprised of nearly 1,100 players forming 72 teams in nine different divisions. The divisions are organized based on age and skill level, ranging from highly competitive to purely recreational. Formed in 1989, this year marks the PSSBL's 30th season of operation. See you at the tryouts!
