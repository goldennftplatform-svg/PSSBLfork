Intro

This post describes a program designed to return an athlete back to throwing following an injury or surgery.  As such, if you are here to read about an off-season throwing program designed to turn your noodle arm into a massive, fire-breathing cannon of baseball manliness, you are out of luck. Throwing is a highly demanding activity. For a throwing athlete attempting to return from injury, getting back to throwing can be difficult. If the athlete attempts to progress too quickly, recovery can be slowed or worse, recurrent or more severe injury can result.
