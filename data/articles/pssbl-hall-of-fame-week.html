Now in our 27th season, the PSSBL has been the home to many outstanding ballplayers. The PSSBL Hall of Fame was established in 2011 to honor those exhibiting exceptional performance on the field, in administrative duties, or both. Each year the PSSBL Hall of Fame Committee receives a considerable number of worthy nominations from around the league, and this year was no exception. The committee members have the very difficult task of narrowing the field to the final list of inductees.

Here is the 2016 Class entering the PSSBL Hall of Fame:

Larry Barlow, Nik Korschinowski, Brian Loucks, Steve Rapkoch, Steve Towey, Joe Townsend

Congratulations to each one of these deserving players! In addition to being premier athletes, they are great ambassadors for the PSSBL and the game of baseball.

Nearly a hundred players, family and friends attended the 2016 Induction Ceremony which took place at Safeco Field June 30th prior to the Mariners-Orioles game.
