The Annual PSSBL Election will be held during an all-players league meeting TONIGHT, Monday, December 2, 2024. Five 2025 Executive Board of Directors positions are voted on by the general membership. Board members serve one-year terms.

The elective positions for 2025 are:  President, Vice-President/Secretary, Treasurer, Player Agent and Fields & Scheduling Director.

These five executive members join the commissioners of each division to sit on the PSSBL Board of Directors. The Division Commissioners are not selected in the General Election, but are voted for during the divisional breakout sessions immediately following the general session.

To nominate yourself or another PSSBL member for a position, please reach out to a member of the 2025 Nominating Committee: Steve Konek, Kirk Harbaugh or Patrick Levens.  Contact information for the committee members was sent via league-wide emails on October16th.    

According to PSSBL Bylaws, any player in good standing — that is, any player who paid dues for the 2024 season in any way and is currently not subject to PSSBL disciplinary action or has an outstanding obligation — is welcome at the 12/2/24 meeting and may vote. Please note proxies may not be voted.  The meeting takes place THIS EVENING, Monday, 12/2/24, 6:30pm, in person at North Bellevue Community Center, 4063 148th Ave NE, Bellevue, WA 98007.  Several PSSBL elections in past years have been decided by a single vote; why not make it yours?
