There has been a great outpouring of tributes, celebrating the life of Joe Townsend, Fields & Scheduling Director for the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League.  Joe passed away unexpectedly April 29, 2025.

                                                       JOE TOWNSEND

Joe Townsend joined the PSSBL in 2002, but his season was interrupted when he was called to duty in Bosnia from August of '02 to September, 2003.  Overall, Joe served four years in the U.S. Army and six years in the National Guard.  

Returning to baseball in 2004, Joe chose to get more involved and served on the PSSBL Board of Directors beginning in 2007.  During his 18+ years on the Board, one of the longest tenures of any board member in league history, Joe was a key contributor in maintaining the PSSBL's dominance as one of the best-run adult sports leagues in the country.  He served as Secretary, Division Commissioner, and from 2016-2025, Fields & Scheduling Director.  Joe was the only person to win the Bob Gilnick Commissioners Award twice as Board Member of the Year.

With a PSSBL career encompassing 21 seasons, Joe excelled in six different divisions: Olympic (Mets, Yankees), Cascade (Cardinals, Phillies, Reds), Adams (Saints, Padres), Sierra (Islanders, Rage, Royals), Smoky (Rangers, Naturals), and Everest (Pilots). His teams won a number of division titles.  Joe Townsend was inducted into the PSSBL Hall of Fame in 2016.   

       2016 PSSBL HALL of FAME INDUCTION at SAFECO FIELD         

In addition to his stellar career stats as a hitter and on the mound, he GM'd a number of teams and played in numerous MSBL World Series in Arizona where he has amassed five championship rings. 

              JOE and WORLD SERIES GATORS TEAMMATES

In 2021, Joe was inducted into the National MSBL Hall of Fame.  Read the story HERE. 

Joe was Head Baseball Coach at Thomas Jefferson High School.   He coached several years in Major League Baseball's RBI program as well as Baseball Beyond Borders, and coached Thomas Jefferson's Girls Bowling Team, advancing to state and national tournaments.  Joe's love of baseball even extended to officiating, working as an umpire for several years.  

                                                                                                                        Baseball Beyond Borders                                                   Umpire Extraordinaire

Beyond baseball, Joe worked in law enforcement with juveniles and did tons of community outreach with young people.  For his tireless work with youth, Joe was awarded King County's MLK Distinguished Medal of Service Award in 2017.

His story made national news which you can read about HERE.  Details regarding any memorials will be forthcoming. Joe's passing leaves a large hole in the heart of the PSSBL and is a tremendous loss to the broader community, but Joe Townsend's legacy will live on and on and on....
