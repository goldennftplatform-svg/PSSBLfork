The PSSBL has updated our liability waiver to account for the COVID-19 pandemic. The following instructions describe the process to re-sign the waiver for those who had registered and signed the waiver prior to 6/17/2020.

Login to your account using the button in the upper right corner of the PSSBL Website.

(Image needed)

After you login, click on your name in the upper right corner to reveal a dropdown menu. Select the link to open the registration screen.

(Image needed)

On the registration screen, select the button to sign the waiver.

(Image needed)

On the resulting screen read the waiver and follow the instructions at the bottom of the screen to complete the process. When complete, you’ll be returned to the registration page. If you no longer see the “View and Sign the Waiver” button, you’re done!
