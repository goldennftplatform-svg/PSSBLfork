1. Games

1.1 Playing Time

1.1.1: Each player in the Adams Division will be guaranteed a minimum of three (3) innings of defense, and one (1) at-bat, per nine-inning (9) game.

No minimum is guaranteed for players who arrive after the first pitch of the game. Teams may set their own guaranteed play policy subject to approval by the Commissioner.

Violations of this rule will result in:

1st Offense - GM Suspended 1 game

2nd Offense - GM Suspended 2 games and wins vacated from both violations

3rd Offense - GM removed from their position.

All violations of this rule are subject to an appeals process.

1.2 Base Runners

1.2.1. A base-runner may have three different types of replacement runners: a substitute runner, the catcher’s substitute, or a pinch-runner.

1.2.2. The purpose of the catcher’s substitute is to speed play in between innings. When there are two outs and the catcher reaches base, the catcher may opt to use a substitute runner. This substitution is optional for the offensive team. It does not need to be agreed to by both managers. There are no running restrictions for the catcher’s substitute runner. It is also legal for the catcher to receive a catcher’s substitute if 2 outs is reached while the catcher is on base.

1.2.3. The purpose of the substitute runner (declared or undeclared) is prevention of injury and/or aggravation of an existing injury.

1.2.4. A team may declare a maximum of two players that are eligible to utilize a substitute runner, should those players reach base safely during the course of the game. These players must be declared to the opposing GM, umpire, and official bookkeeper prior to the first pitch of the game. Once the first pitch has occurred, no players may be designated for eligibility to utilize a substitute runner.

1.2.5. After the game has started, any other substitute runners must be approved by the opposing GM.

1.2.6. The substitute runner must be the last batted out in the lineup.

1.2.7. A substitute runner does not have to be used in every single incident. A player, even though designated to have a substitute runner, may run for himself, if he/she sees fit to do so. If so, he/she shall have no running restrictions.

1.3 Forfeits

1.3.1. A team that forfeits a game will automatically lose its 1st round draft position in the next draft, and the GM will be suspended for one game. For an additional forfeit, the team will lose a buddy pick from the next season, and the GM will be suspended for two games. If no 1st round pick is available due to a previously executed trade, then the 2nd round pick will be used. If no 2nd pick, then 3rd, etc. If there is no draft pick available for use, either because they have been traded away, or the Commissioner determines that the division will not be drafting down to a 3rd or 4th round (or more), then the Commissioner may levy a disciplinary action of his/her own choice.

1.3.2. In the event of two forfeits in a single season, the status of the team and the general manager will be reviewed by the Commissioner, and reported to all other GMs. The team in question will be automatically eliminated from the playoffs, and additional disciplinary action may range from a mandatory resignation of the team’s GM up to a mandatory disbanding of the team. Any additional disciplinary action will be determined by the Division Commissioner and approved by the League Board of Directors.

1.3.3. There are no limitations to the maximum number of players a team may carry on a roster, and plenty of taxi-pool players. Thus, there is no reason to ever forfeit. However, if circumstances are deemed extraordinary, a team’s GM may appeal the penalties in rule 1.6.1 to the Commissioner. This appeal is only possible for a first forfeit.

1.3.4. Any forfeit by a team will subject that team to potential assignment of taxi-pool or other available players to the team’s active roster for the remainder of the season, at the discretion of the Division Commissioner.

2. Player Acquisition

2.1. Buddy picks

2.1.1. The Buddy Pick Season shall start at the first PSSBL General Meeting of the off-season and shall end at the end of the Adams Division regular season.

2.1.2. Buddy Picks may be used at any time during the season defined in 2.1.1, and the team exercising that pick must inform the Commissioner in writing.

2.1.3. Buddy Picks will be awarded to each team at the first General Managers Meeting following the conclusion of the playoffs.

2.1.4. Teams finishing at or above a .667 win percentage shall receive two buddy picks.

2.1.5. Teams finishing below a .667 win percentage shall receive three buddy picks.

2.1.6. The last place team shall receive four buddy picks.

2.1.7. The Commissioner may at any time choose to award an additional buddy pick to a team having less than ten active players on its roster.

2.1.8. The Commissioner may at any time choose to award an additional buddy pick to a team performing poorly that was not the last place team.

2.1.9. Buddy picks may not be traded, transferred, or carried forward to another season.

2.1.10. A buddy picked player may not be traded in the season in which he/she was buddy picked.

2.2. The Draft

2.2.1 Draft order will be determined by playoff seeding, which will take into account overall W-L Records and any needed tie-breakers. Teams will draft in reverse order, with the lowest seeded team drafting first, and the highest seeded team drafting last, per round.

2.2.2 Teams will take turns selecting their draft picks until all teams have declined to participate in a round. Teams are not required to draft any players, provided they already have a minimum roster size of paid/registered players by the end of the draft.

2.2.3 New teams entering the division will be slotted into the draft at the discretion of the Commissioner.

2.2.4 All teams in the division shall have the opportunity to use any picks they own through the end of the 2nd round, including any picks they have acquired via trade. At the conclusion of the 2nd round of the draft, teams who have 17 or more paid players on their roster shall not be eligible to make another pick until all other teams in the division have at least 15 paid roster players. The draft order will remain the same, skipping any teams who have reached the 17 player threshold. Once all teams in the division have at least 15 paid players on their roster, draft order will return to its original form and any team wishing to draft additional players will be eligible to do so. Any team who has reached the 17 player threshold also shall not be allowed to trade their subsequent picks. The purpose of this rule is to help maintain a competitive balance in the division and offset the impacts for teams who have lost multiple players from their roster.

2.3. Immediate Family Members

2.3.1 A family member, for the purpose of this rule, is strictly defined as a parent, sibling, child or spouse.

2.3.2 A team may immediately obtain the family member of a drafted player, and must first spend a buddy pick to do so. If no buddy picks are available, the team will lose its next available draft pick.

2.3.3 A team may add a player to its roster that is an immediate family member of a returning player without expending a Buddy Pick or subjecting the player to the PSSBL draft.

2.4. Taxi-Pool Use

2.4.1 A GM or manager of a team should never allow his team to forfeit a game through being unable to field a team on-time. The penalties are discussed in the Forfeits section of the Adams Division rules, and are severe. The GM has access to the Taxi-Pool of players in order to prevent this from ever being an occurrence, and is expected to know in advance whether or not he will have enough players available and take steps to procure players from the Taxi Pool.

2.4.2 Taxi Pool players are obtained by submitting a Taxi Player Request via the PSSBL website. In the event of an emergency request (24-hours or less until game-time), a team may contact the Player Agent or Commissioner directly and request taxi players. It is at the discretion of the Player Agent or the Commissioner to grant permission for a team to directly contact players not provided through the Taxi Pool system.

2.4.3. All taxi pool players asked to participate in a game will be guaranteed three innings of defensive play and will be in the offensive lineup for the entire course of the game, with the exception of injury.

2.4.4. GMs must report the names of taxi pool players on the lineup card at the beginning of the game, and taxi player statistics shall be entered along with the official score.

2.5. Returning Players and Free Agency

2.5.1. A player is a free agent if he: a) is not a member of any Adams Division team and has not been for a full season, b) has been cut/released by his current Adams Division team, or c) was eligible to be drafted and remained undrafted.

2.5.2. A Returning Player is a player who was an Active/Paid or Inactive/Paid roster member of a team in the previous season and is automatically a roster member for the current season.

2.6. Trades

2.6.1. Trades must be approved by all players involved, both GMs, and the Commissioner, and submitted to the PSSBL Player Agent.

2.6.2. Draft choices are allowed as compensation in trades.

2.6.3. Buddy Picks and Waiver Position may not be used as compensation in trades.

2.6.4. The Commissioner is responsible for ensuring that all trades contain reasonable compensation and do not have a negative impact on the Division.

2.7. Cuts

2.7.1. Cuts shall be made between the end of the season (after the Adams Division Championship game) and the League cut deadline (See League Rules) and do not require approval; only notification (See 2.7.3).

2.7.2. Cuts that are proposed after the League cut deadline (See League Rules) or are proposed during the regular season must be approved by the Division Commissioner. The GM requesting a cut is strongly encouraged to explore trade options or disciplinary action prior to proposing a cut. In this way, all options will have been explored before taking extreme measures.

2.7.3. All cuts must be submitted in writing to the Division Commissioner, PSSBL Player Agent, and the Player to be cut.

2.8. Waivers

2.8.1 The purpose of the waiver process is to allow for the fair placement of new players who become available to the Adams division outside of other player acquisition methods listed above. Once a newly eligible player is identified, they are added to the Waiver List and all GMs are notified by the commissioner of a player's availability via email, at the commissioner's earliest opportunity. This communication will include the player contact information, any available player ability notes, the current waiver claim order and the deadline for submitting a claim.

2.8.2 Review Period and Claims

2.8.2.1 Beginning at the time of this email notification, all GMs have a 5 calendar days Review Period to make a written claim on a player.

2.8.2.2. Claims are not made public. The commissioner is not to disclose which, if any, team makes a claim until the end of the Review Period.

2.8.2.3 The commissioner may choose to shorten the Review Period, at his discretion, in an effort to more quickly place a player, as long as the new player's availability and the shorter Review Period are clearly communicated to all GMs.

2.8.2.4 The commissioner may also process a waiver claim prior to the end of the Review Period, if [all] other teams actively pass on a Waiver Player. This rule is intended to be used only for the purpose of expediting placement of a player (for example – to allow a player to be placed prior to the playoff eligibility deadline or to allow a player to play in a game scheduled prior to the end of the period). The waiver claim period may not be shortened unless all teams in the division have explicitly passed on the player (non-response does not equal pass).

2.8.3 Waiver Processing and Placement

2.8.3.1 At the end of the Review Period, a claimed player will be placed on the claiming team with the highest Waiver Claim Position. That team is immediately placed at the last Waiver Claim Position for the purpose of completing any other claims.

2.8.3.2 In the case where a team claims multiple players at the same time, that team must specify a priority for those players. If a team successfully claims a higher priority player, they will immediately be placed at the last Waiver Claim Position before they may claim a lower priority player.

2.8.3.3 By default, the Waiver Claim Positions are in reverse order of the previous year's standings, regardless of draft position.

2.8.3.4 Waiver Claim Positions may not be traded or exchanged between teams like draft picks and claimed waiver players may not be traded in the same league year that they are acquired.

2.8.3.5 A successful waiver claim neither requires nor costs the team a buddy pick.

2.8.4 Reporting

2.8.4.1 It is the responsibility of the claiming team to vet Waiver Players prior to placing a claim.

2.8.4.2 Claimed players who fail to pay dues and report to their claiming team are not eligible to join any other team in the Adams Division in the current year.

2.8.4.3 A claimed player has 7 days to pay their dues and report to the team (the team, at their discretion, can choose to extend this period).

2.8.4.4 If a claimed player fails to pay dues and report to the claiming team within the 7 day Reporting Period, the claiming team's Waiver Claim Position will be restored, unless that team successfully claims another player within that same Reporting Period.

2.8.4.5 If a claimed player pays dues and reports to the claiming team after the 7 day Reporting Period, it is the team's option, not requirement, to accept the player, and the team's Waiver Claim Position will again be cycled to the last Waiver Claim Position. Or, at that time, the claiming team may choose not to retain the claimed player, maintaining their higher Waiver Claim Position. In this case, the player will be awarded to the next highest claiming team or, if no other claims were made, will re-enter the waiver pool.

3. Post Season

3.1. Playoffs

3.1.1. Playoff seeds are determined by order of regular season finish.

3.1.2. Participation in the playoffs and format shall be determined by the GMs each year.

3.1.3. Tiebreaker procedure (in order):

Number of wins vs. Tied Opponents

Number of Runs Scored vs. Tied Opponents

Coin Flip (2 tied teams) or Rock-Paper-Scissors (3+ tied teams). The Division Commissioner shall attempt to perform this with the tied team GMs in attendance. If the tied team GMs cannot be brought together in time to resolve the tie, then the Commissioner shall break the tie on his own and inform the teams of the outcome.

4. General Division Rules

4.1. New Teams

4.1.1. A new team to the Division must first gain the approval of the Commissioner, and of the PSSBL Board of Directors. Barring a mandate from the Board of Directors, a new team must also receive a majority vote of the Adams Division GMs. In preparing for this vote, GMs should consider the competitiveness of the team in question and ensure that new teams entering the Adams Division will be able to compete effectively.

4.2. General Manager’s Meeting Attendance

4.2.1. Teams are expected to send a representative to every General Managers’ meeting. A General Manager whose team is not present at two Calendar Year meetings will be required to replace the General Manager before the next General Managers’ meeting occurs. If the team fails to select a new General Manager, the Division Commissioner may appoint one for the team.

4.2.3. Only GMs are allowed to vote on Divisional issues. Proxies and/or representatives for a team are not allowed to vote. Exception: The Commissioner may report the vote of a team, provided that it has been submitted to the Commissioner in writing.

4.3. Wood Bat Games

4.4.1. Wood bat games may or may not be played. The number of games to be played with wood bats will be determined each year by a vote of the Divisional GMs.
