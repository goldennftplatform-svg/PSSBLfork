VS.      

For a third time since 2018 the Highline Bears of the Pacific International League challenged the PSSBL to a baseball game.  Wednesday, August 4th, the PSSBL All Stars took on the Bears, who are now in their 6th Pacific International League season.  The All Stars defeated Highline 5-2 at Mel Olson Stadium at Steve Cox Memorial Park in White Center.  Both the PSSBL and the PIL were forced to cancel the 2020 season, so there was no challenge game.  In 2019 our All Stars eked out a 7-5 win.  

 

Pitching, defense and the long ball did the trick this time for the All Stars.  Stephen Bougher, Alex Hatch, Andrew Snook and Barron Tanay combined to strike out 18 Bears batters while allowing just two runs.  Zeb Deede spotted the PSSBL an early lead with a 2-run bomb.  Shane Kessel also went deep.  Maggie Gallagher and Andrew Snook contributed an RBI each.

 

It was a great send-off for PSSBL Fields & Scheduling Director Joe Townsend who managed the PSSBL All Stars.  From 2008 to 2014, PSSBL All Stars played a total of six challenge games against the Everett Merchants of the PIL.  The PSSBL holds a 4-2 edge in that series and is now 7-2 overall versus Pacific International League teams.  Townsend managed all nine PSSBL All Stars teams since '08 and will turn over the reins to a new GM for a next challenge game.  This year he was assisted by coaches Brandon Williams and Casey Bloom.  Congrats Joe Townsend and the 2021 PSSBL All Stars!
