For the first time ever, the PSSBL Draft was held online. All ten divisions drafted over the span of three nights, July 6th, 7th and 8th.  Thanks to all the tryout players who are now members of the non-profit PSSBL. We hope you have a fun season in the league with your new team. Thanks to all the volunteers who helped run the tryouts, to all the GM's and other team representatives, and to the Board members for their tireless efforts.

While every effort was made to place tryout players on a PSSBL team or in a desired division, that was not always possible. Refunds will be forthcoming to the very few players who were not drafted, and partial refunds will be credited to players who paid for a longer season than the one for which they were actually drafted.

As we await the transition to Phase 3 of the state's reopening plan in King, Pierce and Snohomish Counties, the Board of Directors has a plan in place to provide a quality baseball experience for our members.  We are now targeting a late August opening with a 6-week (or possibly even 5-week) season extending to October 4th.  Single game division championship contests would be scheduled October 8-11.

The PSSBL has established "Play Safe" rules so that every participant can stay healthy and safe.  These rules are required by our field providers and expected by our members.  We will constantly monitor the effectiveness of the "Play Safe" rules and moderate them as conditions change or as needed.

In a year of change and challenges, we look forward to getting back on the field!

With 46 teams and over 700 active players, the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League continues to be the premiere adult sports league - not only in the Puget Sound region, but one of the biggest and best in the U.S.
