New Players / General Info

 

I'm new to the league, how do I get started?

Thank you for your interest in the PSSBL.  We're always happy to welcome new players to our league.

There are generally two ways for new players to join the PSSBL - as a Buddy Pick or via the league Tryouts & Draft.

Buddy Picks:

If you know someone who already plays in the league, you can inquire with them to find out if their team needs players (or if they know of a team that needs players).  If so, the manager of that team can send you a buddy pick invitation to join their team.   Once you accept the invitation and register, you become a member of that team.  In this case, there is no need to attend the tryouts - you're on the team.

Tryouts & Draft:

The league holds tryouts every year to help new players find teams - and to help teams find new players.  More details on the tryouts can be found here:  2026 Tryouts as well as in other FAQ articles.

IMPORTANT:   If you are intending to join an existing team as a Buddy Pick - do not register for the draft. 

Once you register for the draft, you are no longer eligible to be a buddy pick.  If you have a question about this, contact the General Manager of the team you plan to join.

 

How / When can I sign up?

Registration for the upcoming season is generally opened in mid-February each year.  The easiest way to tell if registration is open is to look for the Play Ball announcement on the PSSBL home page. 

Note that if the "Sign Up" link is not active, it either means that registration has not yet opened for the upcoming season (usually before mid-February) or registration has already closed for the current season (usually after July 31).  Between the annual draft in late March/early April and the playoff eligibility deadline, the Sign Up link will be directed to the Waiver Wire registration page.

 

Registering for tryouts is a two step process: 

1)   Create a new account - Click here.  

Fill in the requested fields and click submit.

You will receive an email to complete your account creation. 

Click the link in the email, fill in the fields on the resulting page and then click the "Create Your Account" button.

 

If you see this message, your account was created successfully.

 

2)   Sign in and register

Login to your account using the button in the upper right corner of the PSSBL Website.

 

After you login, click on your name in the upper right corner to reveal a dropdown menu.  Select the link to open the registration screen.

 

On the registration screen, select the button to "Register for the 2018 Draft".

Complete the information on the next page, View and Sign the Waiver form, and click the "Register For Draft" button at the bottom.   After that, you'll get a confirmation screen indicating that you've successfully registered for the PSSBL draft.  Congratulations - we'll see you at the tryouts!!

When/where are tryouts and how much do they cost?

The PSSBL holds tryouts every year over 3-4 weekends in March.  Tryouts are intended to help new players find a team to play with and also to help existing teams find players to fill their rosters.  Tryout schedules are usually announced on the league website around mid-January.

Click here for the 2026 Tryout Schedule.

There is no cost to attend tryouts, however you must be registered and paid in full by the Wednesday prior to the draft in order to be eligible to be drafted.  Tryout players will pay the new player dues for a 22-game season ($623.20 in 2025).   If you are drafted onto a team that plays fewer than 22 games, your dues will be adjusted accordingly and the balance refunded.

Historically, nearly all players who attend tryouts and pay in time are able to be drafted onto a team.  In the unlikely event that you are not drafted, your paid dues will be refunded shortly after the conclusion of the draft. 

 

 

How do I know what division to tryout for?

Tryout players do not necessarily tryout out for any particular division.  

When you attend tryouts, you will be trying out for all divisions for which you are age-eligible.  The draft is organized such that players will naturally end up in the division most appropriate for their age and skill level. 

 

 

I play multiple positions, do I have to choose which one to tryout for?

No, there is no need to choose a specific position.  The tryouts are organized in a way to give you plenty of opportunity to demonstrate your ability at multiple positions.  In some cases, players may even be approached by teams during the tryout asking you to try other positions.

 

 

Do I have to attend all of the tryouts?

Generally speaking, the more sessions you make it to the better opportunity you'll have to show teams what you can do - so, players are encouraged to come to as many sessions as you can.

But, don't worry if you have a conflict and can't make one or more of the sessions.  We schedule multiple tryout sessions because we all lead busy lives and not everyone can make it to all the tryouts.   

 

 

I missed the tryouts - can I still play?

The PSSBL is always happy to welcome new players to our league.  How we go about that if you've missed the tryouts will depend on timing...

Before the draft: 

If you can't make it to any of the tryouts but you still want to be eligible for the draft, you can register and pay for the draft via the league website prior to the deadline (scroll up for instructions:  How/When can I sign up). 
We'll also ask you to complete the Paid / Non-tryout Player Profile Form to give teams an idea of your background and abilities.  All players who are registered and fully paid by the deadline, will be eligible to be drafted.


After the draft:   If the draft has already concluded, most teams will already have full rosters but there are still a couple possible ways to get involved for the current season.

Taxi Pool -   The Taxi Pool is a pool (or sub-group) of PSSBL members who wish to be contacted from time to time to play for teams requesting additional players for a particular game.  

The purposes of the Taxi Pool are: a) to ensure that no game is ever forfeited due to a lack of available players on a team, and b) to provide an opportunity for the League's members to play in extra games.   
More details can be found here:  Taxi Pool Policy
 
All members of the PSSBL are eligible to participate in the Taxi Pool, so all that is required is to register and pay the PSSBL base dues of $170.   Taxi pool usage is based on the needs of the teams, so there is no guaranteed number of games a player will be invited to play; however we do prioritize “Taxi-Pool” only players in the selection.  Follow the instructions at the top of this page (How / when can I sign up?) to register for the taxi pool.

Waiver Wire - While all teams already have rosters of 15 or more players by the conclusion of the draft, situations often arise during the season which cause teams to need additional players.  

The league player agent maintains a list of players who wish to be considered as late adds and distributes this list to all teams periodically throughout the season.  Every effort will be made to help players find a team, but understand that it will be on a best effort basis and depends largely on the needs of the teams.

There is no cost to be on the Waiver Wire list.  If you end up placed on a team, you will pay a prorated portion of the player dues based on the number of games remaining in the season (you must be fully registered and paid before you appear in any game).  In order to be eligible for the playoffs, you would need to be on the roster of a team no later than midnight on July 31st.

If you would like to be added to the Waiver Wire list, see the details here: Waiver Wire List - How It Works

I only want to play on the same team as my friend (/ brother / cousin / wife / grandpa / etc...).  Is that possible?

The PSSBL definitely supports friends and family who want to join and play together and we'll work with you to try and help make it happen.   That said, it's sometimes more complicated than it sounds.   

Buddy Picks / Family Picks:

The easiest way for this to happen is if your friend or family member is already a member of a PSSBL team.  In this case, you should work with the General Manager of that team to join their roster.  For competitive reasons, some divisions have limitations on the number of buddy picks they can add in a given season - but in most cases these instances can be accommodated.   

IMPORTANT:   If you are intending to join an existing team as a Buddy or Family Pick - do not register for the draft. 

Once you register for the draft, you are no longer eligible to be a buddy or family pick.  If you have a question about this, contact the General Manager of the team you plan to join.

Tryouts & Draft:

Beginning in 2019, a rule was established to ensure immediate family members can be drafted together.  Immediate family members are defined as siblings, spouses, parents/children or grandparents/grandchildren.  If you and an immediate family member wish to be drafted together, make a note of this in your draft profile (if you have already submitted your draft registration and need to update it, contact support@pssbl.com for assistance).   Note that this could limit the divisions you can be drafted into due to age/experience eligibility restrictions.

If you and another player do not qualify as immediate family members, but are still planning to attend tryouts and enter the draft with the intent to be drafted onto the same team, there are a couple options to consider.  When you submit your draft registration, there is a space to add draft notes.  In these notes, you can indicate their preference to play on the same team.   The player agent will work with division commissioners to make sure all teams are aware of these preferences.  Teams are encouraged to take take these preferences into consideration when making their draft selections and they usually do.  However, these preferences are not binding.

It is very important to understand that once you register and pay for the draft, you can be selected by any team in a division for which you are age-eligible and that team is not obligated to select your friend or family member.   

Another possible option (this is not necessarily a recommendation, just an option) is for one of the two players to register for the draft and include a draft note indicating that they have a friend or family member who would also like to play on the same team.  In this case, the player who registers would attend tryouts and the friend or family member would not.   After the draft, the player selected could work with the GM of the team to have the friend or family member added.   Again, it is important to understand that this preference would not be binding and there is a chance the team who selects the first player may choose not to add the other one.

The bottom line is - we will do our best to accommodate your requests and it often works out, but we simply cannot guarantee it

 

 

.When / Where are the games?

As you can imagine, creating a schedule for 70 teams and 900+ games each year is a monumental task and it requires that the PSSBL schedules games 7 days / wk at fields all across the region.  

Weeknight games normally start around 7PM so players are generally able to get to the field in time to warm up.  At a few fields games start a little earlier due to lighting or curfew issues (never earlier than 6PM). 

Weekend games typically start anywhere from 9AM to 7PM.   Most games last approximately 3 hours.

The PSSBL regular season normally begins around the first weekend in May and finishes by Labor Day with playoffs in September.  Schedules are usually a little bit lighter in May and June and then pick up in July and August as youth leagues wrap up.

PSSBL games are scheduled at the best available fields all across the region.  Our fields are as far south as Tacoma and as far north as Everett.  The vast majority of games are scheduled in King County (either Seattle or the Eastside) with the balance in either Pierce or Snohomish Counties.   Generally speaking, fields at the extreme north and south ends of the region are usually scheduled only on weekends.

When will the schedule come out?

The season schedule is usually published around the middle of April. 

We know you’d like it earlier (so would we!) but unfortunately we have to wait until after the draft to build the schedule because there are often changes to the number of teams on draft day.  Additionally, we have to wait to get our allotment of game dates from the field providers in order to create the schedule.

So… thanks for your patience - we’re working as quickly as we can!

 

  

I haven't played baseball in awhile.  Will I be good enough?

Welcome to the crowd. Almost everyone now playing in the PSSBL had not played for a number of years. If you played some high school baseball, you probably won't have any trouble competing. Actually, there are some good players in the League who were outstanding athletes in other sports, but never played baseball during their youth. Give it a whirl; there is no cost to try out.

 

 

Are metal bats / metal cleats allowed?

Metal Bats:   All PSSBL divisions currently only permit wood bats.

Metal Cleats:   Metal cleats are allowed on some fields, not on others.  Generally speaking, metal cleats are only allowed on all natural (grass/dirt) fields.  Fields with artificial turf usually do not allow metal cleats.   For details on any specific field, see the PSSBL Fields listing.

How does the Waiver Wire process work?

It happens every year!   After the league tryouts and draft have concluded new players contact the league to find out if there’s still a way to join a team for the current season.  Good news - there is! It’s called the Waiver Wire List.   While all teams have a minimum of 15 active players on their roster by the conclusion of the draft, situations often arise during the season which cause teams to need additional players.  Click THIS LINK for more details on how it works.
 

 

I heard that 18 year olds are eligible to play in the PSSBL now - is that right?

You heard right!  The Board of Directors approved a rule allow 18 year old players to participate in the PSSBL beginning in the 2020 season - however, there are some restrictions:

  • Players are NOT allowed on the field or in the dugout at any PSSBL game until they have turned 18.  
  • Players competing on their High School team should not participate until after their HS season concludes. 
  • Players are allowed at tryouts ONLY if they have already turned 18 at the time of the tryout. 
  • Players who turn 18 after tryouts have concluded, can register via the Free Agent tool and will be eligible to join a team once they are 18.
  • Players are eligible to be added as buddy picks in age-eligible divisions once they have turned 18.   They’ll need to wait until they turn 18 to complete registration so, teams should NOT send them the buddy pick invitation early.
