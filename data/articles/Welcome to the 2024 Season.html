9:00 AM - Noon = Tryout Players Age 40 & Over

Noon - 3:00 PM = Tryout Players Age 18-39

The PSSBL is one of the largest adult baseball organizations in the U.S.  Tryouts are FREE - there is no charge to try out for the league.  A league-wide draft will be held March 30th.  Please note players must be at least 18 years of age to participate in any official PSSBL event.

Formed in 1989, the non-profit PSSBL is comprised of over 1,000 players forming 70 teams in ten different divisions.  The divisions are organized based on age and skill level, ranging from advanced to recreational.

For more information contact PSSBL Player Agent Marty Bigelow .
