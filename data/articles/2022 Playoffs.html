The 2022 PSSBL Playoffs are underway!

IMPORTANT NOTE:  Steve Cox Memorial has been closed for the remainder of the season.  

All playoff brackets have been revised as needed and can be viewed directly on this website. On the "Division" page for each of our ten divisions, there is a "Playoffs" tab that shows the respective bracket.

GM's, please make sure to post scores as soon as possible, so the division brackets can be promptly updated.

With a maximum of 104 games to be played in 23 days across the 10 divisions, it's quite the comprehensive schedule.  Thanks to Fields & Scheduling Director Joe Townsend, Web Developer Rich Bonny, and our division commissioners for their work in creating and publishing a solid playoff schedule to conclude our 33rd season.

Several reminders to all teams:  Please tarp natural mounds including all bullpens at the end of the day and lock up fields if we're required to do so when yours is the last game of the day.

Whenever there is threatening weather, call the Rainout line at 425-644-1901 Option 2, check the PSSBL FB page and follow us on Twitter @PSSBLBaseball.
