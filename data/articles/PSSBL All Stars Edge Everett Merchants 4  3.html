The PSSBL All Stars slipped past the Everett Merchants 4-3, Saturday night, July 18th.  The well-attended contest took place at Everett Memorial Stadium, home of the Aqua Sox.   This was the ninth time since 2008 the Merchants of the Pacific International League challenged the PSSBL to a fundraising baseball game. The PIL is one of the premier summer collegiate leagues in the country.  The All Stars now own a 6-3 series edge over the Merchants. The PSSBL played the Highline Bears/DubSea Fish Sticks four times from 2018-2022.  Overall the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League holds a 9-4 series lead over PIL teams. 

 

Shungo Najima of the Cascade Division Nationals started on the mound for the PSSBL. Everett jumped out to a 2-0 lead in the bottom of the first thanks to two All Stars throwing errors.  The All Stars countered with a run in the bottom of the third.  The Merchants added to their lead with a run in the bottom of the fifth, taking a 3-1 advantage.  

 

With the leadoff man on first in the top of the sixth, Spencer Smith slashed a double down the right field line.  Following a walk, the All Stars scored Run #2 on a bases loaded passed ball.  Pinch hitter Jason Rivers promptly lined a base hit to right field scoring the tying run and the eventual game winner.

 

In the ninth the visiting All Stars failed to extend their lead in the top of the frame.  In the bottom of the ninth, closer Adrian Stacy of the Cascade Marlins, pitching a second consecutive inning, worked to protect the slim 4-3 lead.  He walked the leadoff hitter, and with one out the runner stole second and was now in scoring position.  Stacy struck out the final two Merchants batters to seal the All Stars victory.

 

Seven different PSSBL pitchers combined for 8 strikeouts, led by Stacy with 4 K's.  All Stars hurlers yielded just 6 Merchants hits.  Adam Kim got the "W" and Stacy was credited with the Save.  Hitting stars include Joey Clancy who batted 2 for 5 and Jason Rivers, 1 for 1 with 2 RBI including the GWRBI. Other offensive contributors included Shane Kessel and the aforementioned Spencer Smith.

 

Gavin Gehrman, Dylan Jacobs, and Blake Skinner provided the bulk of the offense for the Merchants with a combined 5 for 12, 1 RBI and 2 Runs scored.  Everett pitcher Landon Johnson was tagged with the loss.

 

Rocky Division Commissioner Brandon Williams handled GM duties for the PSSBL All Stars assisted by coaches Casey Bloom and Josh Jacobsen.  The evening began with a ceremonial first pitch from longtime PSSBL player and video game creator Harley Howe.  Milan Coate delivered a beautiful rendition of the National Anthem.  J.D. Panalagao, Darren Smith, and Karl Ramsay handled YouTube Live Play-by-Play and P.A. duties.
