Welcome to the 34th season of the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League!  Online registration for the 2023 PSSBL baseball season is now open.

If you are a Returning Player, log in to your account first, click on your name to get the dropdown menu, select “Registration” and follow the prompts. "Buddy Picks" should respond to a registration email sent directly to you by your GM.

As you proceed through the online registration process, make sure to read the 2023 PSSBL Refund Policy and the PSSBL Waiver which you sign electronically.  If you encounter technical issues as you register, contact support@pssbl.com.

Thanks to Web Developer Rich Bonny for preparing the online 2023 season with a big assist from Michael Willard as well as Jake Liddil.  Have a great 2023 season - Play ball!
