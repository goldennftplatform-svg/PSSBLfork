It’s typically one of the best days of the year – Opening Day of the 2025 season - albeit rather bittersweet this year with the recent loss of Fields & Scheduling Director Joe Townsend. 

PSSBL Baseball is back in the Puget Sound region.  All 714 regular season games of the 2025 season are posted. To view your team’s schedule, go to your team page at https://pssbl.com and click on "Schedule".

If you have a question about tonight's Cascade Brewers vs. Marlins game or any game this season due to threatening weather, call the Rainout Line at 425-644-1901 Option 2. It will be updated as needed.

Please thoroughly review the Field Rules especially since we've added some newly-renovated  and "new-to-us" fields.  Remember, all players who participate in any PSSBL game must be fully paid, Active players and/or eligible Taxi Pool players - all players with waivers signed. Ineligible players who play in a contest will cost their team a forfeit.

Have a good 2025 Season - Play Ball!
