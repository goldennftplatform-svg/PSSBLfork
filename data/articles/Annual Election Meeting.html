The annual PSSBL election will be held during an all-players league meeting TONIGHT, Monday night, December 5, 2022. Six 2023 Executive Board of Directors positions are voted on by the general membership. Board members serve one-year terms.

The elective positions are:  President, Vice-President, Secretary, Treasurer, Player Agent and Fields & Scheduling Director.

These six executive members join the commissioners of each division to sit on the PSSBL Board of Directors. The Division Commissioners are not selected in the General Election, but are voted for during the divisional breakout sessions immediately following the general session.

To nominate yourself or another PSSBL member for a position, please reach out to a member of the 2023 Nominating Committee: Marc Furtick, Steve Konek or Ryan Patrick.  Contact information for the committee members was sent via league-wide emails on October 16th, November 12th, November 28th and December 5th.     

According to PSSBL Bylaws, any player in good standing — that is, any player who paid dues for the 2022 season in any way and is currently not subject to PSSBL disciplinary action or has an outstanding obligation — is welcome at tonight’s 12/5/22 meeting and may vote. Please note proxies may not be voted.  The meeting takes place at 7pm online at Zoom.com.  If you are an eligible PSSBL member, check the Inbox of the email you use to log into your account on the web site.  You'll see a link and passcode to join the Zoom session.   If you don't spot the email, check your Spam or Junk folder.  Several PSSBL elections in past years have been decided by a single vote; why not make it yours?
