The 2018 PSSBL season continues on.

All 765 regular season games of the 2018 season are posted. To view your team’s schedule, go to your team page at https://pssbl.com.  Your season schedule should be the first thing to appear.  If you log in, you can also select My Schedule from the login menu. Anyone playing on multiple teams will see a combined schedule.

Please thoroughly review the Field Rules. Remember, all players who participate in any PSSBL game must be fully paid, Active players and/or eligible Taxi Pool players. Ineligible players who play in a contest will cost their team a forfeit.

If you have a question about a particular game due to threatening weather, call the Rainout Line at 425-644-1901 Option 2. It will be updated as needed.

Have a good, safe, and most of all FUN 2018 baseball season!
