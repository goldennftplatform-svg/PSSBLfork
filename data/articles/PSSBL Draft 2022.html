The 2022 PSSBL Draft takes place Today, Saturday, April 2nd, at Elliott Bay Brewing on Lake City Way and via Zoom.

 

All paid tryout players will be eligible to be drafted at that time.  Once drafted, each tryout player will automatically receive an email welcoming them to their new team.

1:05pm-1:35pm = Sierra Division

1:40pm-2:20pm = Cascade Division

2:25pm-3:05pm = Adams Division

3:10pm-3:35pm = Adirondack Division

3:40pm-4:20pm = Teton Division

4:25pm-4:45pm = Denali Division

4:50pm-Finish = Rocky Division
