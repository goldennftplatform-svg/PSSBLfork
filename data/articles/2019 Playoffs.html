The 2019 PSSBL playoffs are underway.  The playoff brackets can be viewed directly on the website. On the "Division" page for each division, there is a "Playoffs" tab that shows the current bracket.

GM's, please make sure to post scores as soon as possible, so we can publish the next pairing in your bracket.

With a maximum of 123 games to be played in 20 days across the 9 divisions, it's quite the comprehensive schedule.  Thanks to Fields & Scheduling Director Joe Townsend, Web Developer Rich Bonny, and our division commissioners for their work in creating and publishing a solid playoff schedule to conclude our 31st season.

Several reminders to all teams:  Please tarp natural mounds (excluding Bellevue College's mound) including all bullpens at the end of the day and post game results promptly.
