The Puget Sound Senior Baseball League, a non-profit organization, is both innovative and responsive to its members.  For several years an idea has been brewing to create a midweek, midday baseball opportunity for players Age 65 & Over.

This year, in our 35th Season, we made it so.

Through the efforts of many individuals, the PSSBL initiated a new baseball experience in early June.  57 players, divided into three teams, are participating in the new 65+ summer schedule.
