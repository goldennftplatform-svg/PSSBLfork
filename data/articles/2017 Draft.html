The 2017 PSSBL Draft is scheduled for today, Saturday, April 1st, at Sport Restaurant & Bar. Sport is located at KOMO Plaza, 140 4th Ave N, Seattle, WA 98109.

All tryout players who have paid their membership dues to the non-profit PSSBL will be eligible to be drafted today.  

The Final Four of the NCAA Men's Basketball Tournament is the same day, April 1st, and Sport has a lot of big screens and great food.  With two men's basketball teams from the Pacific Northwest competing for the national championship and the PSSBL draft, it should be an exciting day!  

Here is the schedule for the draft - by divisions:

* 12:00pm-12:30pm = Everest

* 12:30pm-1:00pm = Smoky

* 1:00pm-1:30pm = Sierra

* 1:30pm-2:15pm = Cascade

* 2:15pm-3:00pm = Adams

* 3:00pm-3:45pm = Adirondack

* 3:45pm-4:30pm = Teton

* 4:30pm = Rocky

Players who are drafted will be notified promptly by the GM of their new team.  The PSSBL 2017 season will begin the weekend of April 29-30 for most divisions.  Divisions with 18-game seasons may start a few weeks later.
