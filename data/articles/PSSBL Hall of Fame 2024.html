The PSSBL Hall of Fame, established in 2011, welcomes seven new inductees in 2024.  By virtue of their performance on the field over time or tireless work building the league as GM/administrator or a combination of both, these members are honored and thanked.

SCOTT DILLINGER, JEFF MEYER, SCOTT MILLER, ROCKY NORDNESS, BRIAN PATTILLO, DOUG SPRINGFORD, GREG WERRE

Congratulations to each of these new members of the prestigious PSSBL Hall of Fame!  The formal induction ceremony will take place Tuesday, April 30th at T-Mobile Park prior to the Mariners-Braves game.  Watch for ticket information coming soon.
