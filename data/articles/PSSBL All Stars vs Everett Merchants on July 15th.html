The Everett Merchants of the Pacific International League have challenged the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League to a game.  The contest is set for TONIGHT, Tuesday, July 15th at Funko Field in Everett.  First pitch is scheduled for 7:05pm.  This is a fundraiser for the non-profit Merchants team.

From 2008 to 2014 PSSBL All Stars played a total of six charity games against the Merchants.  The PSSBL held a 4-2 advantage in that series.  In 2024 the Merchants edged the PSSBL 7-6 in 13 innings.

From 2018 to 2022 our PSSBL All Stars won three out of four games versus the Highline Bears/DubSea Fish Sticks of the PIL.

 

Overall the PSSBL owns a 7-4 record against Pacific International League teams.

Founded in 1992, the Pacific International League is considered one of the premier Summer Collegiate Baseball organizations in the country.  In addition to the Merchants and Fish Sticks, other teams in the PIL include the Seattle Cheney Studs, Seattle Blackfins, Redmond Dudes, Northwest Honkers, and Gumberoos.

 

PSSBL Commissioners Brandon Williams, Casey Bloom, and Josh Jacobsen are coaching the All Stars.  Special thanks to Steve Konek for helping put the whole event together.  Plans call for a special pre-game tribute honoring the late Joe Townsend, PSSBL Fields & Scheduling Director, longtime Board member, and both PSSBL and National MSBL Hall of Fame member.

 

Admission is FREE!  Donations are encouraged.  Let's have a big show of fan support for our PSSBL players and coaches in Everett.  If you are unable to attend in person, Darren Smith (Smoky Division Naturals & Everest Pilots) will have the play-by-play on our PSSBL Baseball YouTube Channel starting at 6:50 PM.  Go All Stars!
