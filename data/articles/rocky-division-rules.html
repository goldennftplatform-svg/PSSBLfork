1. Minimum Playing Time

1.1. Defense

1.1.1. Minimum Innings

Each player in the Rocky Division will be offered a minimum of four innings of defense per nine-inning game, with at least three of those innings scheduled for the first seven innings. No minimum is guaranteed if the player arrives less than 20min before first pitch of the game, or if a game is shortened (to less than seven innings) by unforeseen circumstances such as inclement weather or darkness.

1.1.2. Position Selection

Defensive position assignments are entirely at the discretion of the General Manager (or a designated Field Manager if applicable).

1.2. Offense

1.2.1. Batting Order

All players in attendance will be included in the batting order. Players arriving after the batting order has been exchanged with the opposing team must be added to the end of the batting order.

1.2.2. Subtracting from the Batting Order

A player may leave the offensive line-up without penalty (to the team) if the player is injured or is forced to leave the game for another commitment. The departing player’s turn at bat will be skipped without recording an out, with the provision that this does not apply where the departing player has been ejected by the umpire. In the case of an ejection, an out will be recorded the first time through the order at the ejected players turn in the order.

1.2.3. Returning to the Batting Order

If a player leaves the offensive lineup for any reason, that player is no longer eligible to return to the game, offensively or defensively.

2. General

2.1. Participation

2.1.1. Participation

Participation is voluntary; players will not be ‘forced’ to play. Each player in attendance will be offered four defensive innings and a place in the batting order, but it is not the intention of the Rocky Division to make a guarantee about all players receiving equal playing time.

2.2. Playing Time Dispute

2.2.1. Playing Time Dispute

Any player who feels their manager has defaulted on their responsibility to provide minimum playing-time has the right to discuss the issue with their General Manager, the League Player Agent, and/or the Rocky Division Commissioner.

3. Pitching

3.1. Inning Maximum

3.1.1. Six Inning Maximum

All pitchers in Rocky Division games are limited to a maximum of six innings per game. All innings pitched by an individual pitcher must be consecutive, regardless of the total number of innings pitched.

3.1.2. Partial Innings

Partial innings pitched for the purposes of this rule are counted toward the five-inning maximum as full innings. For example, a pitcher entering the game with two outs in the first inning (and remaining in the game as the pitcher) has exhausted his/her eligibility after the sixth inning of that game.

3.1.3. Hits Batter Rule

If a pitcher hits four batters in a single game, it will be at the discretion of the opposing GM if s/he(the pitcher in question) shall continue pitching or not in that game. A pitcher so disqualified may remain in the game at another position.

3.2. Additional Limitations

3.2.1. Recommended Rest:

Teams are encouraged to limit pitchers to one game per week, though no limitations beyond the 6 innings per game maximum are currently in place.

4. Courtesy Runners

4.1. Declared and Injured Courtesy

4.1.1. Declared Courtesy Runners:

A maximum of two players requesting courtesy runners can be declared prior to the exchange of line-up cards before a game. The courtesy runner must be the last batted out (unless that player is injured or also a declared player requesting a courtesy runner). In the event that no outs have been recorded in the game for the batting team, the courtesy runner must be the last player in the batting order. Courtesy runners are allowed to steal and have no other limitations.

4.1.2. Injuries:

Injury replacements are allowed in addition to any declared courtesy runners. However, injury replacements are not allowed to steal or advance on passed balls. Injury replacements are only allowed to advance when the ball is put into play by the batter. Injured players must be replaced by the last batted out (unless that player is injured or a declared player requesting a courtesy runner), or by the last player in the batting order if no outs have been recorded in the game for the batting team.

4.2. Catcher Courtesy

4.1.1. Catcher Courtesy Runner:

At the Home Plate meeting GMs from each team can agree with the home plate umpire to have a 2 out catcher courtesy. If the catcher is a base runner when a second out occurs, the GM may call time and replace the catcher with a runner. The rules for that runner are the same as a ‘Declared Courtesy Runner’. (See section 4.1.1)

5. Roster Information

5.1. Roster Additions

5.1.1. General

All players must be added to Rocky Division rosters via the draft, with three possible exceptions: Family members, former players, and buddy picks

5.1.2. Buddy Picks

Each Rocky Division GM has the opportunity to select a ‘buddy pick’ to guarantee that a friend or relative (beyond what’s covered in 5.1.3) can join a team without needing to enter the draft. This buddy pick is not intended to promote ‘ringers’ or players whose talent far exceeds that of the Rocky Division. The amount of buddy picks being either one or two will be determined based on the record of the team from the past season. Teams that have a record over .500 will receive one buddy pick while teams with a below .500 record will receive two buddy picks.

5.1.2.1. Penalty for Buddy Pick Abuse (Ringer Rule)

If at the first GM meeting of the year the GMs agree by a majority vote that a team used their buddy pick to bring in a player who’s skill far exceeds that of the Rocky Division, that team loses their Buddy Pick(s) for the upcoming season and the GM is suspended a minimum of 2 games. The player in question, “The Ringer”, who’s skill far exceeds that of the Rocky Division will be asked to leave the division to go to a more competitive division if they desire to play the following season.

5.1.3. Immediate Family Members

Prior to the league draft, a maximum of two immediate family members of players on a roster can be added to that team’s roster if approved by the team General Manager and the Commissioner. If a pair of family members attends the league tryouts and would like to play on a team together, the first of the two players will require a draft choice, and the second will be added to the roster (without consuming a draft choice). Immediate family members are strictly defined and limited to the legal definition of one of the following: Mothers, fathers, sisters, step-sister, brothers, step-brothers, daughters, step-daughters, sons and/or step-sons.

5.1.4. Former Players

Former players can be added to the roster of their former team after an absence of one or more seasons if approved by the team General Manager and the Commissioner, without regard to payment status of league fees otherwise required to ‘freeze’ a roster spot.

5.2. Roster Subtractions

5.2.1. Cuts

A player must attend a minimum of 33% of their team's completed regular season games in order to guarantee a roster spot for the following season. A completed game is defined in Section 3 of the General PSSBL Rules. Prior to the January 31st PSSBL cut dead line and after the end of the Rocky Division Championship game, each team's general manager will have the option of notifying the Division Commissioner and the Player Agent of their intention to release any players who fail to meet the minimum game attendance. Upon approval from the Division Commissioner, the general manager will then send an e mail to the player and the Division Commissioner notifying the player of the team's intentions to release the player. If the player does not have an e mail address, a certified signature delivery letter may be substituted. Upon receipt of the release notification, the player will have seven (7) days to respond with any intention to appeal their release. The Division Commissioner will then have the option of upholding the player's appeal or forwarding the matter to the PSSBL Board of Directors for further action. The Rocky Division strongly considers general managers to use releasing players for poor attendance only as a last resort for consistent offenders who fail to communicate their absences to their team's general manager within seven (7) days of the absence. Factors including injuries, military service, family emergencies, and work related absences should not be held against a player if these situations were brought to the attention of the general manager in a timely fashion.

5.2.2. Trades for Chemistry

For those rare occasions where team chemistry is negatively affected by the presence of a specific player or players, trades will be strongly encouraged. All trades, however, require the approval of the Commissioner and all players and General Managers involved. In extreme circumstances, if the Rocky Division Commissioner agrees that all other options have been exhausted, a player can be released from a roster following approval from a majority of the General Managers in the Division, and the Rocky Division Commissioner.

5.3. Roster Limitations

Due to the four-inning minimum playing time guarantee for players in the Rocky Division, all Rocky Division rosters will be limited to a maximum of 18 active players). An exception (subject to approval by the Rocky Division Commissioner) for an additional player (or players) can be requested by a General Manager if a team has one or more players on the roster that are also on the active roster of another team in the PSSBL.

5.4. Trades

All trades require the approval of the Commissioner and all players and General Managers involved and must be submitted to the Commissioner in writing.

5.5. Taxi-Pool Players

5.5.1. Applicability of League Rules

All rules listed for Taxi-Pool players in the League Rules under Section 2 also apply to the Rocky Division. except the following minor modifications:

5.5.1.1. Substitute (Taxi-Pool) players can only be obtained by contacting the PSSBL Player Agent and must be approved by the Rocky Commissioner.

5.5.1.2. All players eligible for Taxi-Pool service must have registered for the PSSBL Taxi Pool and paid the fee (if applicable) and must be age eligible for the division.

6. Playoff Information

6.1. Playoff Seeding

Playoff seeds are determined by order of regular season finish and are not affected by playoff game results.

6.2. Tie-breakers

For teams with identical regular-season records, the following tiebreakers apply (in order):

6.2.1. Results of head-to-head competition

6.2.2. Point System For Victories

A point system that provides more points for victories over higher-seeded teams. Example: In an 8-team division, victories over the top seed are worth 8 points while victories over the 8th seed are worth one point. The higher point total earns the higher seed.

6.2.3. Point System For Losses

The same point system applied in reverse for losses within the division.

6.2.4. Coin toss.

7. Rocky Draft

7.1 Draft Order

Draft order is determined by regular season finish and is not affected by playoff results. Teams draft in the reverse order of the regular season standings from the previous season. Teams that are new to the division will be added to the end of each round.

7.1.1 Draft Order Tie breakers

If during the regular season two or more teams finish with the same record, the draft order for the following season will following the same tie breaker rules as defined in section 6.2.

8. Miscellaneous

8.1. General Meeting Attendance

8.1.1. Replacement of GM:

A team that is not represented at two PSSBL General Meetings in any calendar year will be required to replace the General Manager following the second missed meeting, and prior to the next General Meeting of that calendar year.

8.1.2. Additional League Penalties

Additional League penalties apply for any team not represented at a League General Meeting.

8.2. Forfeits

8.2.1. First Forfeit:

A forfeit by any Rocky Division team during the season will result in a warning issued to the teams GM by the Rocky Commissioner.

8.2.2. Second Forfeit:

A second forfeit during the season by any Rocky Division team will result in exclusion from the playoffs and mandatory replacement of the General Manager prior to the next General Meeting of that calendar year.

8.2.3. Additional League Penalties:

Additional League penalties may apply for any team forced to forfeit a regular season or playoff game at the discretion of the Rocky Commissioner.

8.3. Division Naming Convention

Minor League team names (current or former) are required for all new Rocky Division teams unless an exception is approved by a majority vote of the General Managers in the division and by the PSSBL Board of Directors. In addition, new team names are limited to those that have not been used by any current or former Major League teams, and are not currently in use by another member of the PSSBL

8.4. Wood Bat Usage

Wood bats can be used by any player at their discretion during a game. GMs from opposing teams can mutually agree to have a game be an all wood bat game prior to the start of the game.
