There may be no crying in baseball, but it is with great sadness that the Board of Directors announces the cancellation of the 2020 PSSBL Season.

Entering the third week of August, King, Pierce, and Snohomish Counties remain in Phase 2 of the state's reopening plan due to the COVID-19 pandemic.  As a league, we are out of time to schedule even an abbreviated season.  The health and safety of our members, their families, friends, neighbors, co-workers, etc. is paramount.

Dues-paying members will be receiving refunds or may direct the League to carry over and apply their dues to the 2021 Season.  All League members should carefully review important information included in the August 19th email from the PSSBL League President.

Questions, concerns, and other input can be directed to Executive Board members and/or Division Commissioners.  Contact information is available HERE.

Stay healthy.  Stay safe.  See you on the field for the 2021 Season!
