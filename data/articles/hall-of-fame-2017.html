Now in our 29th season, the PSSBL has been the home to many outstanding ballplayers.  The PSSBL Hall of Fame was established in 2011 to honor those exhibiting exceptional performance on the field, in administrative duties, or both.  Each year the PSSBL Hall of Fame Committee receives a considerable number of worthy nominations from around the league, and this year was no exception.  The committee members have the very difficult task of narrowing the field to the final list of inductees. 

Here is the 2017 Class entering the PSSBL Hall of Fame:

Curtis Vinson, Gabe Orendorff, John Olsen, John Brandhagen, Tom Evans Krause, Mike Collier

Congratulations to each one of these deserving players!  In addition to being premier athletes, they are great ambassadors for the PSSBL and the game of baseball.  They join 38 players previously inducted into “The Hall”.

The 2017 Induction Ceremony will take place at Safeco Field on Thursday, May 4th.
