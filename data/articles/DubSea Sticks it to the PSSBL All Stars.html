VS.      

For a forth time since 2018 the DubSea Fish Sticks (formerly the Highline Bears) challenged the PSSBL to a baseball game.  Friday, July 29th, the Fish Sticks, now in their 7th Pacific International League season, edged the PSSBL All Stars 5-4.  The well-attended contest took place at Mel Olson Stadium at Steve Cox Memorial Park in White Center.  The All Stars still own a 3-1 series edge over the Fish Sticks.

 

The PSSBL jumped out to a 2-0 lead in the first inning thanks to a sac fly by Isaiah Williams and an RBI single from Josh Benson.  Nolan Watson got the start for the All Stars and struck out the side in the first inning.  A Kenny McCormick double and RBI single from Ronel Trias pushed the visitors lead to 3-0 in the second frame.

 

The Fish Sticks struck back in the third with two unearned runs off Jake Fredrick, narrowing the margin to 3-2.  In the top of the fifth, Anthony Ulskey drove in a run for a 4-2 All Stars advantage.  Barron Tanay allowed two runs in the bottom of the fifth, and DubSea tied the game.  The Fish Sticks scored the eventual winning run in the sixth off Ryan Patrick, who was tagged with the loss.

 

PSSBL pitchers combined for 12 strikeouts.  Hitting stars include the aforementioned Trias, McCormack, Ulskey, and Benson, along with Johnny Locher (2-3, SB) and Aaron Rose (1-2, SB).  The All Stars were plagued by a lack of clutch hitting, stranding 18 baserunners over 9 innings.

 

From 2008 to 2014, PSSBL All Stars played a total of six challenge games against the Everett Merchants of the PIL.  The PSSBL holds a 4-2 edge in that series and is now 7-3 overall versus Pacific International League teams. 

 

Rocky Division Commissioner Brandon Williams took the GM reins from PSSBL Fields & Scheduling Director Joe Townsend who managed the PSSBL All Stars from 2008-2021.   Williams was assisted by coaches Marty Bigelow, Casey Bloom and Josh Jacobsen.
