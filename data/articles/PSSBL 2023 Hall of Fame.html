Since its founding in 1989 the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League has been the home to many outstanding ballplayers.  The PSSBL Hall of Fame was established in 2011 to honor those exhibiting exceptional performance on the field, in administrative duties, or both.

Now in our 34th season, the 2023 Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony took place in the Ellis Pavilion at T-Mobile Park on May 26th.  Over 100 friends, family members and teammates turned out to honor this year's inductees.  The ceremony was followed by a buffet and the Seattle Mariners game against the Pittsburgh Pirates.

 

Congratulations to each one of these deserving players!  In addition to being premier athletes, they are great ambassadors for the PSSBL and the game of baseball.  They join 73 players previously inducted into “The Hall”.
