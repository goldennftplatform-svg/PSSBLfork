The non-profit Puget Sound Senior Baseball League (PSSBL), one of the largest amateur adult baseball leagues in the U.S., plans open tryouts for all baseball players, age 18 to 65+, in preparation for the 2021 season.

Players must be at least 18 years of age to participate in any official PSSBL event. Tryouts are FREE - there is no charge to try out for the league.  Register now online!

Remaining tryout schedule:

  • Saturday, March 27, 2021 - Bellevue College
  • Sunday, March 28, 2021 - Russell Road Park in Kent

In order to minimize the number of people at the field at any given time, tryouts will be conducted in small groups by appointment only.  Registered tryout players will be contacted by the Player Agent to schedule their tryout session.  Please carefully read the articles below in their entirety titled "2021 PSSBL Tryout Plan - How it Works" and "2021 Tryouts - What to Expect".

 

An online league-wide draft will be held Saturday, April 3rd.

 

The non-profit PSSBL is comprised of over 1,000 players forming 65 teams in ten different divisions. The divisions are organized based on age and skill level, ranging from advanced to recreational. Formed in 1989, this year marks the PSSBL's 32nd season.

 

For more information contact PSSBL Player Agent  Jake Liddil .
