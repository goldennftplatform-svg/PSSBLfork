Continuing a long tradition, the PSSBL will be represented at the Hutch Award Luncheon at Safeco Field.  The 2018 event, which runs from noon-1:30pm on Wednesday, January 24th, will feature Hall of Fame pitcher Randy Johnson as the keynote speaker.  Check-in and Silent Auction begin at 11:30am.  The program begins at 12-noon.

Read more about “The Big Unit” Randy Johnson here:  https://www.fredhutch.org/en/events/hutch-award/speaker.html

Texas Rangers pitcher Jake Diekman is being honored as this year’s Hutch Award recipient.  Here's some interesting info about Diekman:  https://www.fredhutch.org/en/events/hutch-award/winner.html

Due to renovation of the playing surface, attendees will dine in a Safeco Field suite which accommodates 20 guests.  We'd like the suite to be full of PSSBL members and guests, so if you are interested in reserving a spot, please contact Tom Evans Krause right away.  The minimum suggested donation is $150.
