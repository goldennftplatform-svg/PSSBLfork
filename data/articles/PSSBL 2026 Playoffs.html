The 2026 League-wide Playoffs are winding down!

All playoff brackets can be viewed directly on this website. On the "Division" page for each of our ten divisions, there is a "Playoffs" tab that shows the respective bracket.  Hint: Clear your browser history when checking for updates.

Several reminders to all teams:  Scores should be reported asap so brackets can be updated promptly. Please tarp natural mounds including all bullpens at the end of the day and lock up fields if we're required to do so when yours is the last game of the day. Continue to follow all field rules as we do during the regular season.

With a maximum of 119 games to be played in 23 days across the 10 divisions, it's quite the comprehensive schedule.  Thanks to Fields & Scheduling Director Anthony Hill, VP Paul Davison, Web Team Members Rich Bonny, Michael Willard, Josh Emery, & Christian Cardenas, and our Division Commissioners for their work in creating and publishing a solid playoff schedule to conclude our 37th season.
