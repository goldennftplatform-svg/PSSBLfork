I forgot my password - can you help?

Go to the login screen and click on the “Forgot Password” link.  This will open a page allowing you to reset your password.  

Once you enter your email address and click the “Request Password Reset” button, you will receive an email with further instructions.  

Follow those instructions to complete your password reset.

 

I tried to reset my password, but I didn’t get the email (or I lost it) - what do I do?

Here are a few things to try:

  • Check your junk mail folder to make sure it wasn’t delivered there.

  • Make sure you have the right email address associated with your account (your GM can check this from the team roster management screen).

  • You can also try submitting another password reset request.

  • If none of the above works, contact support@pssbl.com for help.

 

I have a new email address (or address / phone number / etc…) - how do I update my profile on the website?

You can edit any of your profile information from the player profile screen.  Click on the login button in the upper right corner of the website and sign in using your existing account credentials.  After logging in, click your name in the upper right corner and select profile & preferences from the drop down menu.

This will bring up a screen similar to below.  Click the Edit button and edit the details in your profile as appropriate.  Note that you can also update your password from this same screen.

 

 

I don’t remember what email I used to register - can you help?

Your General Manager has access to this information on the team’s “Manage Roster” page.   

If you are still having trouble, contact support@pssbl.com for help.

 

When will registration open for the new season?

Registration for the new season typically opens in mid-February each year.   

Look for an announcement prominently displayed on the website.

 

I don’t remember how to register - can you help?

Returning players can click on the login button in the upper right corner of the website and sign in using your existing account credentials.  After logging in, click your name in the upper right corner and select registration from the drop down menu.

That will take you to a registration screen similar to below.  Select the appropriate status for each of your eligible returning teams, complete the Taxi Pool form, sign the waiver and then select the button to complete the registration.   You can choose to “pay now” or you can just register and return at a later time to pay.

 

 

I’m playing on two teams, but only want to pay for one right now - what do I do?

The best way to handle this is to only register as “Active” on the team you want to pay for now.   You can return at a later date and update your status to active on other teams and your account balance will be adjusted accordingly at that time.  

 

When do I need to pay?

Here are a few relevant dates to be aware of:

March 28:    Deadline for the discounted returning player dues

March 29:    Deadline for all teams must have at least 15 active/paid players on their roster

May 3:  Opening Weekend - players must be paid in full before participating in a PSSBL game.

July 31:  Must be active on the roster and paid in full in order to be eligible for the postseason

For additional information, please see the “When must these dues be paid?” section on the About The PSSBL webpage.

 

I’m changing teams for this season, how do I register for my new team?

In order to join a new team, you’ll need the GM of that team to send you a buddy pick invitation before you can register on their roster.  Once the GM sends the invitation, you should receive an email inviting you to join that team.

 

I was invited as a buddy pick.  What do I do now?

When you’re invited to join a team as a buddy pick, you’ll receive an email with a link to accept the invitation.  Click the link and follow the instructions on the website to register with your new team. If you haven’t received the email, check with the GM of the team to confirm they sent it to the correct email address.  They can also re-send the invitation if necessary.

If you’re still having trouble, contact support@pssbl.com for help.

 

When will the schedule come out?

The schedule for the new season is usually published around the second week in April.  

We know you’d like it earlier (so would we!) but unfortunately we have to wait until after the draft to build the schedule because there are often changes to the number of teams on draft day.  Additionally, we have to wait to get our allotment of game dates from the field providers in order to create the schedule.

So… thanks for your patience - we’re working as quickly as we can!

 

I have a friend that wants to play - what do I do?

If you think your friend would be a good fit on your team, you should check with your GM about adding to your team as a buddy pick.  

If he’s not a fit for your team (or if your team doesn’t have any buddy picks they can use on him), you can direct him to register for the PSSBL tryouts and new player draft  https://pssbl.com/#!/signup.  

Or, if the tryouts and draft have already occurred, you can direct him to register as a Free Agent (link pending - contact Player Agent in the meantime).

 

Why aren’t my stats showing up on the webpage?

Most likely because your GM (or whoever is delegated to enter stats on your team) hasn’t entered them yet.  If this is a persistent problem and is upsetting to you, maybe offer to take on that role for your team.

 

I got injured (or deployed / or transferred jobs) and can’t play the rest of the season - can I get a refund?

Good news!  The PSSBL is one of the few adult sports leagues in the country that allows refunds.  There are some restrictions, however. Please see the Refund Policy for details.

 

What’s a Taxi Pool?

The Taxi Pool is a subset of PSSBL players who wish to be contacted periodically to fill in for teams who need additional players for a particular game.  The intent of the Taxi Pool is to minimize the chance of a team having to forfeit due to lack of players - and also to give our league members the opportunity to play in additional games throughout the season.  

Visit this link for more information: https://pssbl.com/#!/articles/TaxiPolicy

 

How do I register / unregister for the Taxi Pool?

You can change your Taxi Pool registration settings at any time on the Registration page.  

Scroll up to the “I don’t remember how to register - can you help?” section for registration instructions.

 

I’m registered for the Taxi Pool, but I’ll on vacation for the next couple weeks - what should I do?

If you’ll be out of town or otherwise unavailable to participate in the Taxi Pool for awhile, it’s best to unregister from the Taxi Pool for that time.  Not only will this stop you from receiving a bunch of emails for games you can’t attend - it will also increase the chances that requests get filled because they will only be sent to players who might actually be around.   You can change your Taxi Pool registration settings at any time on the Registration page.

 

I’m a returning player, but I took a year off - why am I being charged the new player dues?

As detailed on the Player Rights page:   A "Returning Player" is defined as a player who paid dues in some form, Active, Inactive, or Taxi Pool Only in the prior year.  

Also, returning players must be registered by the “Early Registration” deadline in order to be eligible for the reduced returning player dues.  For more details about the costs, see the Player Dues section on the About the PSSBL webpage.

If after reading this, you still believe you are being charged the wrong amount, please email info@pssbl.com.

 

I’m a returning player, but I have an injury and won’t be able to play at the start of the season.  What should I do about registration?

If you’re certain you’ll be able to play at some point in the year, your best option to pay the least amount possible will be to register as Inactive and pay your base membership dues by the Early Registration deadline (this ensures you’ll pay the lower returning player amount).   

Then, when you’re back and ready to play, login and change your registration status to “Active”.  The prorated per game portion of your dues will be calculated at this point allowing you to pay for just the games remaining on the schedule.

If you’re not certain you will be able to play this season, you may want to consider just registering as Inactive but not paying up front.  This could ultimately cost you a little bit more since you’ll miss the Early Registration deadline - but it also reduces the risk that you’ll pay and be unable to play.   If you decide later that you’re healthy enough to play, you can update your registration status to Active and pay the prorated portion at that time.
