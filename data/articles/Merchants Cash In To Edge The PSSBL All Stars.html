It took 13 innings but the Everett Merchants eked out a win over the PSSBL All Stars, 7-6, Saturday night, June 1st.  This was the seventh time since 2008, the Merchants of the Pacific International League challenged the PSSBL to a baseball game. The well-attended contest took place at Funko Field in Everett, home of the Aqua Sox.  The All Stars still own a 4-3 series edge over the Merchants.  The All Stars played the Highline Bears/DubSea Fish Sticks four times from 2018-2022.  Overall the PSSBL holds a 7-4 series lead over PIL teams. 

 

The PSSBL jumped out to a 2-0 lead in the first inning, but the Merchants quickly tied the game with two runs in the bottom of the frame.  The All Stars regained the lead 4-2 with single tallies in the third and fourth innings.  However, the Merchants put up a 3-spot in the bottom of the fourth and led 5-4.  

 

Some feel the top of the sixth inning was the key to the game as the All Stars loaded the bases with no outs.  No damage was done as the Everett boys wriggled off the hook, leaving all runners stranded and no PSSBL runs scored. The All Stars managed to push across a run in the seventh inning to tie the game.  It remained knotted up through the eighth and ninth innings.

 

The tightly contested game moved into extra innings.  Both teams scored a single run in the tenth and the game continued.  The Merchants pushed across the winning run in the bottom of the thirteenth inning off Marty Bigelow, who was tagged with the loss.  Reider Vane picked up the "W" for the Merchants.

 

PSSBL pitchers combined for 10 strikeouts, led by Barron Tanay with 3 K's over 2 innings pitched.  Hitting stars include Joseph Clancy who went 5 for 7 with a RBI and 2 Runs and PSSBL Hall of Famer Paul Fisher, who batted 2 for 4 with 2 RBI.

 

Aidan Van Rensum was the hitting star for the Merchants with 3 Hits in 4 AB's, 1 Run and a RBI.  Aidan is the son of longtime star PSSBL player Steve Van Rensum, who was in attendance at the game.

 

Rocky Division Commissioner Brandon Williams handled GM duties for the PSSBL All Stars assisted by coaches Marty Bigelow, Casey Bloom and Josh Jacobsen.  PSSBL & National MSBL Hall of Famer Joe Townsend, who managed the All Stars from 2008-2021, and legendary Merchants GM Harold Pyatte, a member of the National Baseball Congress Hall of Fame, threw out ceremonial first pitches.
