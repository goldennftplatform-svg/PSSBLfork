The Everett Merchants of the Pacific International League have challenged the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League to a game.  The contest is TONIGHT, Saturday, July 18th, at Funko Field in Everett.  First pitch is scheduled for 6:35pm.  This is a fundraiser for the non-profit Merchants team.

From 2008 to 2014 PSSBL All Stars played a total of six charity games against the Merchants.  The PSSBL held a 4-2 advantage in that series.  Over the last two years the teams traded wins by similar 7-6 scores, the Merchants prevailing in 2024 and the PSSBL victorious in 2025.

 

From 2018 to 2022 our PSSBL All Stars won three out of four games versus the Highline Bears/DubSea Fish Sticks of the PIL.  Overall the PSSBL owns an 8-4 record against Pacific International League teams.

 

Founded in 1992, the Pacific International League is considered one of the premier Summer Collegiate Baseball organizations in the country.  In addition to the Merchants and Fish Sticks, other teams in the PIL include the Seattle Cheney Studs, Seattle Blackfins, Redmond Dudes, and the Northwest Honkers.

 

PSSBL Commissioners Brandon Williams, Casey Bloom, and Josh Jacobsen are coaching the All Stars.  Special thanks to Steve Konek for helping put the whole event together.
