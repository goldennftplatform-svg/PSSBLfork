The PSSBL Hall of Fame, established in 2011, welcomes eight new inductees in 2023.  By virtue of their performance on the field over time or tireless work building the league as GM/administrator or a combination of both, these members are honored and thanked.

PAUL DAVISION, STAN FRIEDLANDER, DOUG HARRIS, ERIC KOOSMANN, JAKE LIDDIL, DON MORAN, KELLY RYAN, TOD SAKAI

Congratulations to each of these new members of the prestigious PSSBL Hall of Fame!  The formal induction ceremony will take place Friday, May 26th at T-Mobile Park prior to the Mariners-Pirates game.  Watch for ticket information coming soon.
