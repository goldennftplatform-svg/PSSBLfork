For the first time in four years a team from the Pacific International League challenged the PSSBL to a baseball game.  From 2008 to 2014, PSSBL All Stars played a total of six charity games against the Everett Merchants of the PIL.  The PSSBL holds a 4-2 edge in that series.

Thursday, August 9th, our PSSBL All Stars took on the Highline Bears, who are now in their 4th PIL season.  The All Stars defeated the Bears 6-4 at the Bears home field, Mel Olson Stadium at Steve Cox Memorial Park in White Center. 

PSSBL Fields & Scheduling Director Joe Townsend managed the PSSBL All Stars.  Corey Wiggins was the hitting star for the PSSBL, going 3-4 with a single, double and home run.
