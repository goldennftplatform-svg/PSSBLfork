What is one of the rarest achievements in baseball since the game entered the Modern Era?  Perhaps you guessed it - the Perfect Game.  Since 1904 there have only been 21 Perfect Games officially registered by Major League Baseball.  Overall, Perfect Games amount to .0001053% of all MLB games ever played - even dating back to the late 1800's.

A Perfect Game is a game in which a pitcher completes a minimum of nine innings with no batter from the opposing team reaching any base. To achieve a perfect game, a team must not allow an opposing player to reach base by any means: no hits, walks, hit batsmen, uncaught third strikes, catcher's or fielder's interference, or fielding errors; in short, "27 up, 27 down" for a nine-inning game.

Wednesday, June 15, 2022, history was made in the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League. LHP Max Bayern of the Sierra Division Rage threw a complete Perfect Game against the rival Sierra Saints.  The 30 & Over Sierra Division-leading Rage cruised to a 9-0 win behind Bayern's memorable performance.

    MAX BAYERN, PSSBL RAGE

Ironically, the Saints had tagged the Rage with their only loss of the season up until now.  The 31-year old Bayern was scheduled to only go 5 or 6 innings, but as the game progressed, no runners had reached first base and the hurler was dealing.  The southpaw only reached a 3-ball count one time in the entire 9-inning contest, while striking out 11 batters.  Bayern pounded the zone and the Rage defense made every play behind him, including a diving catch in left field.  Here's the line:

The first "Modern Era" Perfect Game was attributed to Cy Young in 1904.  The last MLB Perfect Game was registered by Seattle's own Félix Hernández on August 15, 2012, as the Mariners defeated Tampa Bay 1-0.

Now Max Bayern has a Perfecto on his resumé.  The lefty joined the PSSBL in 2021.  In slightly more than a season, he has thrown 80.2 IP over 19 games, giving up a mere 42 hits, striking out 136 while only walking 5.  Bayern's ERA and WHIP are microscopic at 0.892 and 0.607 respectively.

Clearly a force to be reckoned with now and for seasons to come, congratulations Max Bayern of the Sierra Rage on your historic accomplishment!
