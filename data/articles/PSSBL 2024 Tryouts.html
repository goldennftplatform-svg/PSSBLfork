FINAL TRYOUTS TODAY - SUNDAY, MARCH 24th - Interlake High School, 16245 NE 24th St, Bellevue, WA 98008

9:00 AM - Noon = Tryout Players Age 40 & Over

Noon - 3:00 PM = Tryout Players Age 18-39

The non-profit Puget Sound Senior Baseball League (PSSBL), one of the largest amateur adult baseball leagues in the U.S., is holding open tryouts for all baseball players, age 18 to 65+, in preparation for the 2024 season.

PLAYERS AGE 40 & OVER ARE STRONGLY ENCOURAGED - WE NEED YOU!

All tryouts are on turf fields, so no metal cleats, please.

Players must be at least 18 years of age to participate in any official PSSBL event. Tryouts are FREE - there is no charge to try out for the league.  Online registration is now open.

A league-wide draft will be held Saturday, March 30th.  All tryout players who wish to be drafted must pay membership dues by Friday, March 29th.

The PSSBL is comprised of nearly 1,100 players forming 70 teams in ten different divisions. The divisions are organized based on age and skill level, ranging from advanced to recreational. Formed in 1989, this year marks the PSSBL's 35th season.

For more information contact PSSBL Player Agent Marty Bigelow .
