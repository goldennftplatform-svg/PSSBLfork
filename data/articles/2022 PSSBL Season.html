The 2022 season is underway and PSSBL baseball is back in the Puget Sound region!

All 674 regular season games of the 2022 season are posted. To view your team’s schedule, go to your team page at https://pssbl.com and select "Schedule".  If you're logged into your account, you can select "My Schedule" from the login menu. Anyone playing on multiple teams will see a convenient combined schedule.

Please thoroughly review the Field Rules. Remember, all players who participate in any PSSBL game must be fully paid, Active players and/or eligible Taxi Pool players with waiver signed. Ineligible players who play in a contest will cost their team a forfeit.

If you have a question about a particular game due to threatening weather, call the Rainout Line at 425-644-1901 Option 2. It will be updated as needed.

Have a good, safe, and most of all FUN 2022 baseball season!
