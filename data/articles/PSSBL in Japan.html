A number of PSSBL players recently played Baseball 日本野球 and enjoyed a cultural exchange in Japan.  The trip was organized by longtime PSSBL member Hiro Kida (Rainier Cubs, Teton RiverCats & Adirondack Solar Sox). 

PSSBL players included Stuart Chernis, Jeff Droker, Harley Howe, Scott McHugh, Paul Schnarr, Jerome Watson, Dave Winkel, and host Hiro Kida.

                       

The group visited Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto, Hiroshima and Nara.  During the 12-day trip, our PSSBL team toured pagodas, ancient castles and World Heritage (UNESCO) sites.  They rode bullet trains, enjoyed nightlife in the busy cities, and played some baseball against local teams in Hyōgo Prefecture.

The PSSBL players traveled with Isao Machii, a real Samarai Warrior, who holds six Guiness World Records of Katana Iaidō.  Machii shared many insights into Japanese heritage and customs.
