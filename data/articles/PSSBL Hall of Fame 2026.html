The PSSBL Hall of Fame, established in 2011, welcomes seven new inductees in 2026.  By virtue of their performance on the field over time or tireless work building the league as GM/administrator or a combination of both, these members are honored and thanked.

CASEY BLOOM, NIKOLAI BROWN, CORY COATE, MARSE McNAUGHTON, TOSHI NIWA, JIM RADOVICH, SPENCER SEABOURNE

Congratulations to each of these new members of the prestigious PSSBL Hall of Fame!  The formal induction ceremony takes place TONIGHT, Friday, May 15th at T-Mobile Park in the Ellis Pavilion prior to the Mariners-Padres game.  Tickets for Edgar's Home Run Porch are sold out, but you can view the event live on our PSSBL YouTube Channel starting at 5:15 PM, by clicking HERE.  While you're there, be sure to subscribe to our channel.
