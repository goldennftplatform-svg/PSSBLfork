For the second consecutive year the Highline Bears of the Pacific International League challenged the PSSBL to a baseball game.  Last year our All Stars eked out a 6-4 win.

Thursday, August 1st, the PSSBL All Stars took on the Bears, who are now in their 5th PIL season.  The All Stars defeated the Bears 7-5 at Mel Olson Stadium at Steve Cox Memorial Park in White Center. 

PSSBL Fields & Scheduling Director Joe Townsend managed the PSSBL All Stars.  Joe Clancy of the Cascade Diamondbacks was the hitting star for the PSSBL, going 3-4 with a single, two doubles and two RBI.

From 2008 to 2014, PSSBL All Stars played a total of six challenge games against the Everett Merchants of the PIL.  The PSSBL holds a 4-2 edge in that series and is now 6-2 overall versus Pacific International League teams.
