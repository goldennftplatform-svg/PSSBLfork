If you are using the Google Chrome browser and have been experiencing very slow loading times or difficulty playing videos on the draft profile pages, you may be affected by a glitch in the browser. There is an experimental protocol called "QUIC" that may be interfering with timely communication with the server. The recommended remedies are:

  1. Use a different browser (e.g. Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  2. Disable the QUIC protocol in your Chrome browser

Instructions for Disabling QUIC Protocol:

1) Enter "chrome://flags" in your browser address bar and hit enter

2) Scroll down to the setting for "Experimental QUIC protocol"

3) Choose "Disabled" as the setting.

4) Restart the browser. You should then be good to go moving forward.
