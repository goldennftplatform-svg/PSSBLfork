The Puget Sound Senior Baseball League held a social media video contest for the first time ever in 2024.  Many teams and individuals took up the challenge of submitting PSSBL-related videos which were featured on Instagram.  Contest rules restricted entries to active league members who followed our Instagram account and utilized the official hashtags #PSSBLBASEBALL2024 and #PSSBLBASEBALL.

Besides rewarding our members for showing off the prowess in hitting, catching, throwing, running bases and creativity, the PSSBL sought to increase the number of followers and activity on our IG account.  Engagement rose 50% during 2024 thanks in large part to the contest and everyone's participation.

After the competition closed September 30th, a panel of judges selected from the PSSBL Board of Directors reviewed the entries and determined the winners.

FIRST PLACE - Denali Giants for "Championship Highlights" & the "3 Amigos Reel".  Prize: One Paid 2025 Membership
