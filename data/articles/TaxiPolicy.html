Definition & Purpose

The Taxi Pool is, simply, a pool (or sub-group) of PSSBL members who wish to be contacted from time to time to play for teams requesting additional players for a particular game.

The purposes of the Taxi Pool are: a) to ensure that no game is ever forfeited due to a lack of available players on a team, and b) to provide an opportunity for the League's members to play in extra games.

General Managers may find instructions on submitting a Taxi Pool request on the FAQ/Help page.

Qualifications

The Taxi Pool is open for sign up from the time a season's registration begins through the end of the season. All registered and paid members, who have signed the League's liability waiver, are eligible to sign up regardless of ability, experience, or the like (refer to Player's Rights policy). The League requests that you sign up only if you are likely to be available when called upon, and you wish to participate over and above your regular PSSBL team schedule. A member not in good standing with the League is not eligible for the Taxi Pool duty. Injured players should never accept a Taxi Request and are encouraged to temporarily unregister from the Taxi Pool until healthy.

Cost & Dues

A member who has registered and paid for a full season's dues for a team is eligible to also participate in the Taxi Pool in the same season by requesting this upon registration for the season, or by modifying his account at any time. No additional dues are assessed for this privilege.

A member, who has registered and paid their base membership dues but has not paid for a team roster spot and only wishes to participate in the Taxi Pool for a season, is eligible to do so. The base membership dues are currently $170 ($130 for returning players who pay by the deadline). Taxi Pool dues are subject to annual review.

Administration

The Taxi Pool is maintained and governed by the PSSBL Board for the benefit of the PSSBL, and management for the Taxi Pool currently is the responsibility of the League Player Agent. All questions should be directed to the League Player Agent.

Restrictions & Limitations

Registration for the Taxi Pool is not a guarantee that you will be called upon to play during a particular season. However, every attempt will be made to ensure that Taxi Pool names will be distributed in a deliberate and equitable fashion through the Online Taxi Pool System.

As stated in the PSSBL Rules (Section: Other, paragraph 5), a Taxi Pool player may not pitch unless agreed to by the Division Commissioner AND the opposing team's GM or field manager prior to the start of the game.   Taxi Players are not eligible to play in the Playoffs.

A Taxi Pool player will not become a roster add-on for a team, unless approved by the League Player Agent and the Divisional Commissioner, and only after paying the appropriate pro-rated League dues.

A Taxi Pool player may only play for a team in a division in which he is age eligible. Examples: a 30-year old will not be offered to Sierra, Teton, or Smoky division teams, but may be offered to all other divisions; a 40-year old will not be offered to Smoky division teams but may be offered to all other divisions.    (A special exception is made for the Rainier division due to the low number of available taxi pool catchers). 

A player should never appear in a game as a Taxi Player unless they have been assigned through the Online Taxi Pool System, or they have the approval of both the Division Commissioner AND the opposing team's GM.  Players and GMs found violating this rule will be subject to discipline from the league.

Player's Responsibilities

Players who choose to participate in the Taxi Pool are responsible for registering themselves.  You can find instructions on our FAQ/Help page.

If you accept a request, you are expected to show up and play in that game as the requesting team is counting on your presence.  Should your situation change after you accept the request, you are expected to notify the requesting GM and the Player Agent as soon as possible so they can make alternate arrangements.

You will not be required to wear a team uniform or colors, unless you choose to do so. You must attempt to wear baseball attire, bring your own equipment, and to have footwear appropriate for the field conditions.

You must follow all PSSBL rules of play and conduct, and all field-specific rules. If you do not, you will be removed from the Taxi Pool.

If you will not be available to play as Taxi Pool player for an extended period, we ask that you unregister from the Taxi Pool for that duration and then re-register when you return.  This will help reduce the number Taxi Pool requests sent to players who are unavailable to play - and thus will increase the likelihood that the request will be filled.

Catchers

Players who can play the Catcher position are most in demand when vacations or injuries take their toll during the season. Taxi Pool players must designate in their profile if they are willing and able to play this position. Those who can catch will likely be called more often than those who cannot. Catcher is the only position that can specifically be requested by a manager accessing the Taxi Pool.

Timing of Requests

Taxi Pool Player Requests should be made at least 48 hours in advance. To increase the chances of finding an available player, GM's should try to plan their team's roster for games a week or more ahead of time.

The League strongly urges managers to bring out more than 9 players for every game. Traffic, car problems, injuries, or any unexpected event could occur at the last moment, reducing the number of players who show up for your team. There could also be injuries during the game. As a general guideline, it is recommended that you request one or more Taxi Pool players if you have less than 11 confirmed players for a game. Ultimately, a GM knows what is best for his team.

Making Taxi Pool Player Requests

Taxi Pool players may only be obtained by submitting a request through the PSSBL online Taxi Pool system.   When the request is generated, it will be sent to a list of 25 players.  Requests are filled on a first come, first served basis.  If you're having trouble getting your request filled, see this section on our FAQ/Help page. 

While we always recommend planning ahead and requesting Taxi Pool players at least 48 hours in advance, we understand sometimes unforeseen situations arise.  Use these instructions if you find yourself in this situation.

Reminder - Taxi Pool players are not allowed to pitch, unless agreed to by the Division Commissioner AND the opposing team’s GM or manager prior to the start of the game.

How it Works for Players

As a Taxi Pool player, you will receive emails periodically inviting you to participate in a game as a Taxi Pool player.  The email invitation will include the game details and a link to accept or decline the request.  Simply select one of those two links to record your response.  We request you decline requests that you cannot make because it helps the GM, Commissioner and Player Agent determine if the request needs to be expanded.  Players should respond as promptly as possible as requests are accepted on a first come, first served basis.  If you select the link to accept the request that has already been filled, the resulting screen will inform you of that.  

Once you have accepted and been assigned to a Taxi Pool request, you will receive an email from the league website informing you of the game details and also providing contact information for the requesting GM.  If you only wish to play specific positions, or have other limitations, you should mention that to the manager as soon as possible.

The online Taxi Pool request system has been designed to prioritize players who play in the same and/or similar divisions as the request was generated for.  However, if you do not think you can compete at that division's level of play, you may choose to defer the request to play. The PSSBL does not wish for you to play in a situation where you may be in over your head.  A member whose team has a game at different field within 4 hours of the Taxi Pool request will be filtered out of the list.  

How it Works for Managers

Taxi Pool requests should ALWAYS be submitted through the online Taxi Pool system.  While the vast majority of requests made through the online Taxi Pool system are filled, occasionally they are not.  In these cases, the manager should pursue the following options:

  • If a sufficient amount of time has passed without the request being filled, you can "expand" the request. 
  • If the game time is approaching (especially if it's less than 24 hours away) and the request has not been filled, the GM should contact their division commissioner (cc: to the opposing GM and the Player Agent) to request assistance identifying a player.   (Note: this should NEVER be the first choice - always submit a request through the online Taxi Pool system first).

Use of Ineligible Players

General Managers are responsible to utilize the Taxi Pool as it is intended, and to never abuse the system.  Under no circumstances should a GM allow a substitute player obtained through means other than the Taxi Pool system to take the field without agreement from both the Division Commissioner and the opposing GM.  Failure to adhere to this policy will be considered use of an ineligible player and may lead to disciplinary action as described in section 7.7 of the league rules.
