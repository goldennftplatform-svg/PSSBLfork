For the second year in a row the Highline Bears of the Pacific International League have challenged the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League to a game.  The contest is set for TONIGHT Thursday, August 1st.  First pitch is scheduled for 7:05pm.

 

It’s VS. Cancer Night, as the Bears host the PSSBL in this charity game match up.  $5 of every ticket purchased will be donated to the VS. Cancer Pediatric Brain Tumor Foundation & Seattle Children’s Hospital.

Last year our PSSBL All Stars defeated the Bears 6-4.   The Bears, who are now in their 5th PIL season, play at Mel Olson Stadium at Steve Cox Memorial Park in White Center (1321 SW 102nd St, Seattle, WA 98146).

 

From 2008 to 2014, PSSBL All Stars played a total of six charity games against the Everett Merchants of the PIL.  The PSSBL holds a 4-2 edge in that series and a 5-2 record overall against PIL teams.

 

PSSBL Fields & Scheduling Director Joe Townsend will manage the PSSBL All Stars.  For more information, including ticket prices, check out the Bears website at http://highlinebears.com/.
