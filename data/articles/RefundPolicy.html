PSSBL Player Dues Refund Policy for 2026

Once a player has paid his/her annual dues to become a member of the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League, the League will not refund dues to players who refuse to play for the team they are drafted by.

Once the PSSBL draft begins, refunds shall be granted for the following exceptions:

  1. A player is not drafted or placed on a team roster prior to the start of the season.
  2. A player is drafted into a division for which he/she has overpaid.  The refund consists of the difference between dues amount paid and division dues where a player is drafted.
  3. Military deployment on a pro-rated, per game basis.

Refunds may be granted after the start of the regular season for the following exception:

  1. Season-ending injury on a pro-rated, per game basis.

The deadline for refund requests is 11:59:59  pm on May 31, 2026. Following the PSSBL draft, granting a player's refund request must not cause his/her team to fall below the minimum roster of 15 Paid, Active players. Once the regular season begins, only the "Per Game" portion of the player's dues will be refunded. The "Base Dues" portion will be retained by the League, and the player will remain a "Member in Good Standing" (Inactive). The player may not return as an Active player and/or Taxi Pool player during the same season he/she received a refund.

 

The League Treasurer and respective Division Commissioner will make a final determination on each refund request.

Approved by the PSSBL Board of Directors Jan 20, 2026.
