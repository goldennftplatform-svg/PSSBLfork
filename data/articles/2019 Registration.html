Welcome to the 31st season of the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League!  Online registration for the 2019 PSSBL baseball season is now open.

If you are a returning player, log in to your account first, click on your name to get the dropdown menu, select “Registration” and follow the prompts. "Buddy Picks" should respond to a registration email sent directly to you by your GM.

New tryout players are strongly encouraged to sign up online using the Sign Up button in the "Register for Tryouts" box adjacent to this article.  Registration for new players entering the draft is also possible at the check-in table on tryout days, but online registration on our website is preferable. The tryouts are set for March 10th at Interlake High School in Bellevue, March 17th at Russell Road Park in Kent, March 23rd at Bellevue College, and March 31st at Interlake High School.  For more information, read the article above entitled "PSSBL Tryouts 2019". 

As you proceed through the online registration process, make sure to read the 2019 PSSBL Refund Policy and the PSSBL Waiver which you sign electronically.  If you encounter technical issues as you register, contact support@pssbl.com.

Thanks to Web Developer Rich Bonny for preparing the online 2019 season with an assist from Michael Willard, Jake Liddil and Jason Yormark.  Have a great 2019 season - Play ball!
