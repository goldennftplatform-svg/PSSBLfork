The SEATTLE GIANTS, piloted by Toraj Nowkhasteh, recently took home the hardware in the 25 & Over National Division at MSBL's 2025 Desert Classic in Palm Springs.

The team forged a 4-1 record during the tourney, ultimately outscoring their opponents 49-44.  The Giants avenged their only pool play loss - to the Los Angeles River Rats - in the championship game, beating the River Rats 15-7.

   Seattle Giants

Jake Casson tossed a complete game against L.A. to secure the title, striking out nine along the way.

Offensive stars included Kenny McCormick II who had 4 hits and 2 RBI and Sean Clancy who had 3 hits, 3 runs and 2 stolen bases. Other major contributors to the "O" were Sam Ballweg, Josh Benson, Jade Braff and Josh Schiefer.
