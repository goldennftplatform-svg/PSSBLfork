For the third time since 2018 the Highline Bears of the Pacific International League have challenged the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League to a game.  The contest is set for TONIGHT, Wednesday, August 4th.  First pitch is scheduled for 7:05pm.

 

In 2019 our PSSBL All Stars defeated the Bears 7-5.   Both the PSSBL and the PIL were forced to cancel the 2020 season.  The Bears, who are now in their 6th PIL season, play at Mel Olson Stadium at Steve Cox Memorial Park in White Center (1321 SW 102nd St, Seattle, WA 98146).

 

From 2008 to 2014, PSSBL All Stars played a total of six charity games against the Everett Merchants of the PIL.  The PSSBL holds a 4-2 edge in that series and a 6-2 record overall against PIL teams.

 

PSSBL Fields & Scheduling Director Joe Townsend will manage the PSSBL All Stars with assistant coaches Casey Bloom and Brandon Williams.  For more information check out the Bears website at http://highlinebears.com/.
