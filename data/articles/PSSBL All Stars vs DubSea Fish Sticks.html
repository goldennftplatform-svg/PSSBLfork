For the fourth time since 2018 the DubSea Fish Sticks (formerly the Highline Bears) of the Pacific International League have challenged the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League to a game.  The contest is set for TONIGHT, Friday, July 29th.  First pitch is scheduled for 7:05pm.  Tickets are $12 for Adults and $8 for Senior/Military/Youth.

 

In 2021 our PSSBL All Stars defeated the Bears 5-2.   The Fish Sticks, who rebranded during the off-season, are now in their 7th PIL campaign.  Home field is Mel Olson Stadium at Steve Cox Memorial Park in White Center (1321 SW 102nd St, Seattle, WA 98146).  Mel Olson Stadium has been nicknamed "The Fryer" by the team.

 

Founded in 1992, the Pacific International League is considered one of the premier Summer Collegiate Baseball organizations in the country.  Other teams in the PIL include the Seattle Cheney Studs, Seattle Blackfins, Redmond Dudes, Northwest Honkers and Everett Merchants.

 

From 2008 to 2014, PSSBL All Stars played a total of six charity games against the Everett Merchants.  The PSSBL holds a 4-2 edge in that series and a 7-2 record overall against PIL teams.

 

PSSBL Rocky Division Commissioner Brandon Williams will manage the PSSBL All Stars with assistant coaches Casey Bloom, Josh Jacobsen and Marty Bigelow.  For more information check out the Fish Sticks website at https://gofishsticks.com/

 

Let's have a big show of fan support for our PSSBL players and coaches Tonight.  Go All Stars!
