A record 21 PSSBL-based teams participated in national post-season play in 2023 either in Arizona, Florida or both.  In another record-setting performance nine Puget Sound Senior Baseball League teams made it to the championship game in their respective division. 

Ultimately two teams came home with the hardware. Capitalizing on their earlier Las Vegas Kickoff Classic championship, the 60+ Envision Northwest Emeralds, claimed the MSBL 60+ National Division World Series title.  The Emeralds defeated Boston Wolfpack at Tempe Diablo Stadium 5-4.

    60+ National Champions - Envision Northwest Emeralds (Not Pictured: Alan Gibson)

In Fort Myers, Florida, the Washington Titans claimed a title in the AAAA Timeless Division at the Roy Hobbs World Series.  The 70+ Titans defeated longtime nemesis Minnesota Bees 12-11.  The championship contest began at Lee County Stadium, spring training home of the Minnesota Twins.  After a rain squall washed out the field in the 6th inning, the game was moved to the Boston Red Sox facility, JetBlue Park for the conclusion.

                                         70+ National Champions - Washington Titans

In addition, several PSSBL players won World Series rings with teams from outside the PNW.  Congrats to all our champions - way to represent the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League!
