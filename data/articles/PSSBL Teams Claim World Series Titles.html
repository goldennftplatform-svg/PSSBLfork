The Puget Sound Senior Baseball League is now recognized as the #1 largest adult baseball league in the USA, and for the first time since 2007 four different PSSBL-based teams won World Series titles in the same year.

A record 23 PSSBL teams participated in national post-season play in 2025 either in Arizona, Florida or both.  Quite a few of our teams made it to the playoffs and at least seven played a championship game in their respective division. 

Ultimately four teams came home with the hardware.

Capitalizing on their earlier MSBL Desert Classic championship, the Seattle Giants, claimed the MSBL 25+ Cactus Division World Series title.  The Giants defeated the Chicago Devils at the Tempe Diablo Complex 8-2.

                                           25+ Cactus Champions - Seattle Giants

Special Mention: Earlier in the 25 & Over playoffs, Seattle Ravens pitcher Jacob Sesso tossed a no-hitter, beating the Alaska 49'ers 9-0.

     Jacob Sesso flanked by catchers Brian Perry and Cody Reed

Meanwhile, the 35 & Over Seattle Ravens, claimed their first MSBL Cactus Division World Series title.  The Ravens defeated the Houston Gamblers 10-6 at Tempe Diablo Stadium, spring training home of the Los Angeles Angels.

                                               35+ Cactus Champions - Seattle Ravens

The 60+ Seattle Pilots accomplished a rare feat.  Having won in 2024, the team scored back-to-back titles with a victory over the Colorado Falcons in the 2025 MSBL Desert Division World Series.  The Pilots defeated the Falcons 14-4 at Tempe Diablo Complex.

                                             60+ Desert Champions - Seattle Pilots

In Fort Myers, Florida, the Washington Titans claimed the crown in the AAAA Timeless Division at the Roy Hobbs World Series.  The 70+ Titans defeated the South Jersey Spikes 4-2.  The championship contest was held at Lee Health Stadium, spring training home of the Minnesota Twins.

                                   70+ National AAAA Champions - Washington Titans

In addition, more than a dozen PSSBL players won World Series rings with teams from outside the PNW.  Congrats to all our champions - way to represent the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League!
