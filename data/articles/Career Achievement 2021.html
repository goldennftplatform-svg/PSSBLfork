Congratulations and a well deserved 2021 Career Achievement Award to PSSBL player/manager and baseball ambassador Bruce Morrison.  Bruce has brought people together to experience adult baseball for 20+ years.  Playing with his son in the Teton Division and his old buddies in the Adirondack where since 2014 Bruce compiled a 20-4 record in the division, Bruce has been "The Man". 

 

With his traveling teams to tournaments Bruce always brings a positive and fun attitude that is contagious!  He has played several years at the MSBL World Series in 55’s, 60’s, 65’s and 70’s division as well and the Father-Son and Bruce even added Grandson to the Father-Son name!  Pretty cool to have three generations on the diamond. 

 

Bruce's name is added to an esteemed list of those PSSBL members presented the Career Achievement Award.  2014 - Jim Fleming; 2015 - Ken Combs; 2016 - "Seattle Stu" Fitelson; 2017 - Jerry Duris; 2018 - Larry Webb; 2019 - Al Manter; 2020 - Bob Sorenson

  

Well done 2021 Career Achievement Award winner Bruce Morrison!
