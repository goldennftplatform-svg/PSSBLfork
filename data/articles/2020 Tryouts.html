COVID-19 UPDATE: The PSSBL is resuming limited activities under Washington State's Phase 2 Reopening plan.

The non-profit Puget Sound Senior Baseball League (PSSBL), one of the largest amateur adult baseball leagues in the U.S., plans open tryouts for all baseball players, age 18 to 65+, in preparation for the 2020 season.

Remaining tryout schedule:

  • Sunday, June 28th - Steve Cox Memorial Park in White Center

In order to minimize the number of people at the field at any given time, tryouts will be conducted in small groups by appointment only.  Registered tryout players have been contacted by the Player Agent to schedule their tryout session.  Please carefully read the articles below in their entirety titled "2020 PSSBL Tryout Plan - How it Works" and "2020 Tryouts - What to Expect".

 

Players must be at least 18 years of age to participate in any official PSSBL event. Tryouts are FREE - there is no charge to try out for the league.

 

An online league-wide draft is tentatively set for the week of July 6th.

 

The non-profit PSSBL is comprised of over 1,000 players forming 70 teams in ten different divisions. The divisions are organized based on age and skill level, ranging from advanced to recreational. Formed in 1989, this year marks the PSSBL's 32nd season of operation.

 

Watch for further updates regarding plans for the 2020 season.
