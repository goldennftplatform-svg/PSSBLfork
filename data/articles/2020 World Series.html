Despite the fact the league was forced to cancel the local 2020 baseball season, a number of PSSBL-based teams excelled in national World Series play this fall.  Six teams made it to the playoffs in their respective divisions with the 45 & Over Puget Sound Knights, 65 & Over Washington Titans, and the Father-Son 3N2 Rangers all making it to the semi-finals.

The Puget Sound Twins claimed a national championship at the MSBL World Series in Arizona.  The 45 & Over Cactus Division team compiled an 8-1 record during the first week of November wrapping up the title with a 7-2 victory over the San Diego Diamond Dawgs in the final.  Read the complete story HERE.
