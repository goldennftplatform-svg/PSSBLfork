Once in a great while, a PSSBL player has a career day.  That was the case recently for Mike Lindberg.  Lindberg, who has been a member of the PSSBL since 2012 had the kind of day most ballplayers only dream of.

Lindberg, who plays in several divisions including Everest, Rainier and Adirondack, recently hit for the cycle.  Not only that, but as his 5th hit of the day, racked up a second triple, a rarity at any level of baseball.  That's right.  In the same game Lindberg hit a single, double, HR and not one, but two triples versus the Wizards at Bellevue College.

This has happened only one time at the major league level: https://www.nonohitters.com/2016/09/07/evers-hits-majors-only-two-triple-cycle-66-years-ago-today/

Lindberg continues to excel at the highest level with the 50+ Everest Steelheads, 60+ Rainier Pilots and 60+ Adirondack Osprey, posting big offensive numbers.  In addition to being a high level SS defender, he sports an 16-1 record on the mound for his several teams in 2021.
