The 2018 PSSBL regular season schedule is published. As usual it takes a monumental effort to put together a 71-team, 9 division, 765-game regular season schedule. Factors such as field quality, available days/times, spreading out a team’s schedule over four months, umpire group assignments, daylight/sunset issues, requested blackout dates, avoiding back-to-back-to-back dates, and other conflicts must be considered.

 

Key factors this year include the creation of the new 60+ Rainier Division, the addition of 8 more teams compared to last year, and the extension of the Everest Division schedule to 24 games.  Adding it all up, we’re playing 89 more regular season games than we did in 2017.

 

The PSSBL regular season gets underway the weekend of April 28-29. To view your schedule, simply click on your team page at https://pssbl.com.
