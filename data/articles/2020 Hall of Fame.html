The PSSBL Hall of Fame, established in 2011, welcomes seven new inductees in 2020.  By virtue of their performance on the field over time or tireless work building the league as GM/administrator or a combination of both, these members are honored and thanked.

MARLIN APPELWICK, MARI-JO FRASER, ALAN GIBSON, DAVE "DOC" KUECHLE, DAVE MATHEWS, BRIAN MORRISON, RICK PARK

Congratulations to each of these new members of the prestigious PSSBL Hall of Fame!  We hope to have an official public induction ceremony later this summer if and when political and health officials give the OK to resume communal gatherings. Details are pending.
