It happens every year!  

After the league tryouts and draft have concluded new players contact the league to find out if there’s still a way to join a team for the current season.  Good news - there is!  It’s called the Waiver Wire.   While all teams have a minimum of 15 active players on their roster by the conclusion of the draft, situations often arise during the season which cause teams to need additional players. 

The league maintains a list of players who wish to be considered as Waiver Wire players and makes this list available to all teams throughout the season.  Every effort is made to help players find a team, but understand that it will be on a best effort basis and depends largely on the needs of the teams.

There is no cost to be on the Waiver Wire list.  If a player ends up being placed on a team, they pay a prorated portion of the player dues based on the number of games remaining in the season (players must be fully registered and paid before appearing in any game).  In order to be eligible for the playoffs, players need to be on the roster of a team and fully paid no later than midnight on July 31st.

IMPORTANT NOTE:  The Waiver Wire is NOT intended for existing players looking for a new or additional team.  Players on existing rosters who would like to find another team, should contact the Player Agent and/or the Commissioner of the division(s) they would like to play in.

How it works for players:

To be added to the Waiver Wire list, follow the two step process below to sign up.

Once a player has completed the sign up process, the league Player Agent will review the player’s profile details, addresses any questions and approve adding the player to the Waiver Wire List.   Upon approval by the Player Agent, an email will be sent to all teams in the age-appropriate divisions to inform them that a new Waiver Wire player is available.  This is the start of the 96-Hour Waiver Wire claim window.

During the 96-Hour Waiver Wire claim window, teams may contact the players to get more information or even invite them to a practice.  Players are encouraged to engage with these teams as it’s a great way to determine if they’re a fit for the team - but, it’s important to understand that Waiver Wire players are not part of the team unless/until they are added to the team’s roster via the claim process.

After the 96-Hour claim window:

If one or more claims have been made, the league player agent will work with the relevant division commissioners to confirm which team has issued the successful claim and then will inform both the player and the team.    

If no claims are made in the 96-Hour window, any team in any age-eligible division can claim the player at any time and the claim will be immediately assigned to that team.

Once a claim has been confirmed, the team will then issue an invitation for the player to join the team.  When the player accepts the invitation and completes their season registration and payment and they’ll be added to the team’s roster and will be eligible to play.

Waiver Wire players are under no obligation to join the team.   However, it is important to understand that if a Waiver Wire player declines to join the team they are assigned to via the claims process, they will not be eligible to join any other team’s roster that season.

How to register for the Waiver Wire

Step 1:  Create an account

  • Go to this link and fill out the requested fields at the bottom of the page to request an account https://pssbl.com/#!/signup

  • You will receive an email to complete your account creation.  

  • Click the link in the email, fill in the fields on the resulting page and then click the "Create Your Account" button.

Step 2:  Register for the Waiver Wire List

  • Login to the PSSBL website  https://pssbl.com/#!/login

  • Click on your name in the upper right corner and select the Registration menu option from the drop down list

  • On the registration screen, click the button to Register for the Waiver Wire for the 20XX Season

  • Complete the information on the registration screen and click the button at the bottom to Register for the Waiver Wire.  

  • Be sure to add any notes about your baseball background or anything else that you want teams to know about you.   You can also send a picture of yourself to support@pssbl.com and we’ll add it to your Waiver Wire profile.   We’ve found players who provide this additional detail have a much better chance of being picked up by a team.

     

How it works for teams:

General Managers will receive an email when a new Waiver Wire player who is age eligible for their division becomes available.  The GM can login to the PSSBL website to view the player’s profile page. In the player agent notes section, there will be a notation identifying when the 96-Hour claim period ends. 

GMs are encouraged to contact prospective Waiver Wire players to determine if they are a fit - and even invite them to a practice or workout if desired.   If/when the GM decides a Waiver Wire player is someone they would like on their team, they can select the “Place Claim” button on profile page for that player to log their claim.  

Important notes about making claims:

  1. GMs are expected to do their diligence before making a claim.  When you make a claim, you are committing to have that player on your roster for the season and providing them reasonable playing time as you would anyone else on your roster.   Placing a claim on a Waiver Wire player in order to block them from another team is not an acceptable practice.
  2. If the GM makes a claim within the 96-Hour claim period, the claim will be logged - however making a claim does not guarantee the claim will be awarded to that team.   See below for the Waiver Wire claim priority by division.
  3. Understand that even if a Waiver Wire claim is confirmed, the player is not obligated to actually register and pay for the season.   However, that player will only be eligible to play for the team to which they were assigned via the Waiver Wire process.

After the 96-Hour claim window:

At the end of the 96-Hour claim period, the Player Agent will confirm the claim with the relevant division commissioner(s).   If no claims are made in the 96-Hour window, any team in any age-eligible division can claim the player at any time and the claim will be immediately assigned to that team.

Once a claim has been confirmed, the Player Agent will communicate the confirmed claim to the GM and the player and provide instructions for adding the player to the team’s roster.  Note that for divisions with limited buddy picks, a Waiver Wire claim does not count towards the team’s buddy pick limit.

Free Agent Claim Priority:

The divisional Waiver Wire claim prioritization was modeled after the league draft priority.  

Claims on eligible Waiver Wire players within the 96-Hour claim period will be evaluated based on the following priority order (subject to age eligibility):

  • Competitive divisions first - oldest to youngest.  
  • Intermediate divisions next - oldest to youngest
  • Recreational divisions - oldest to youngest.

Within those divisions, claims will be evaluated based on the divisional Waiver Priority order maintained by the Division Commissioner.  

n some circumstances, a claim may be confirmed prior to the end of the 96-Hour claim period.  This will only happen under one of the following conditions:

  1. A claim is placed by a team and there is no possibility of another team making a claim with higher priority.
  2. A claim is placed and all teams with higher priority confirm with the division commissioner that they do not intend to place a claim.
