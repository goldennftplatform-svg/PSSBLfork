The 2025 League-wide playoffs are underway!

All playoff brackets can be viewed directly on this website. On the "Division" page for each of our ten divisions, there is a "Playoffs" tab that shows the division-specific bracket.

                                    

Several reminders to all teams:  Scores should be reported asap so brackets can be updated promptly. Please tarp natural mounds including all bullpens at the end of the day and lock up fields if we're required to do so when yours is the last PSSBL game of the day.

With a maximum of 116 playoff games to be played in 24 days across the 10 divisions, it's quite the comprehensive schedule.  Thanks to VP Paul Davison, Web Developer Rich Bonny & the Web Team, and our division commissioners for their work in creating and publishing a solid September schedule to conclude our 36th season.  Memorial Note: Most of the available field dates for the 2025 playoffs were arranged early this year by our late Fields & Scheduling Director Joe Townsend, whose legacy lives on.

Reminder: Whenever there is threatening weather, call the Rainout line at 425-644-1901 Option 2, check the PSSBL FB page and follow us on X @PSSBLBaseball.

Good luck to all our teams in the playoffs!
