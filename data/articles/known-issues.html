PSSBL Website – Known Issues

Last Updated: 2/22 9:30 PM

Below is a list of known issues on the new website.  If you encounter an issue not addressed below, please report it to support@pssbl.com  so we can investigate further.

  1. Some emails (buddy pick invitations, new account requests, etc…) are taking longer than expected to be delivered.    The emails are all being sent, but delivery is slow.  We are working to resolve this issue as soon as possible.   If more than 12 hours has passed and you still don’t receive the email, please check your junk mail folder to make sure it wasn’t flagged as spam by your service provider.  And if you don’t find it there either, please report the issue to support@pssbl.com for further investigation. UPDATE: We have installed new mail delivery software and upgraded authentication procedures, so delivery of these mails should be much improved. Issue is considered resolved.

 

  1. In certain cases, after accepting a buddy pick invitation the player is still not able to register for the team.   We are working on a fix to resolve this issue as soon as possible.  In the meantime, if you need to complete registration right away, please contact support@pssbl.com with the team, player’s name and player’s email address and we will provide assistance. Update: Fixes to the buddy pick system were installed today. Buddy picks should be able to accept invitations and register with their new teams. GMs can resend invitation mail, but not more than once every 12 hours.

 

  1. After completing registration, GMs may need to log out and log back in before they are able to access their roster management functions.

 

  1. From mobile devices, the menus do not go away after selecting the desired menu option.  Until this issue is resolved, the workaround is to refresh the page.(or just tap anywhere on the page outside the menu area)

 

  1. Current balances are not showing up on the team roster pages – and therefore GMs are unable to determine which payers have paid.  Also, the active roster size is not reporting correctly (another manifestation of the same issue).  We are working to resolve this issue. UPDATE: Balances are now showing on roster pages. Active and inactive roster counts appearing on the division pages has also been corrected. Issue resolved.

 

 

In addition, the following functionality is coming soon:

  1. The sorting, filtering, viewing of player details and other functionality on the list of tryout players still in progress – these items, as well as the draft administration tool will be finished in advance of the draft. Update 2/20: These features should be available by the end of this week.

 

  1. The taxi pool request & management tools will be completed and instructions provided prior to the regular season.
