Since 1989 the PSSBL has been the home to many outstanding ballplayers.  The PSSBL Hall of Fame was established in 2011 to honor those exhibiting exceptional performance on the field, in administrative duties, or both.

The combined 2020-2021 Induction Ceremony takes place at Hotel Interurban, 223 Andover Park E. in Tukwila, TONIGHT, Saturday, July 24th from 6-9pm.  YOU'RE INVITED!   We'll have a buffet with lots of hot and cold apps plus sweets and soft drinks.  There will be a cash bar and parking is free.  Cost is $40 per person for the whole package.

 

WALK-UPS AT THE DOOR ARE WELCOME.  Let's have a big showing of our membership to celebrate the achievements of the very impressive Hall of Fame Classes of 2020 and 2021!

 

 We announced a 2020 Class last year, but could not have an induction ceremony due to the pandemic.  The 2021 HOF Class was announced in April of this year.

 

Here is the 2020 Class entering the PSSBL Hall of Fame:

Marlin Appelwick, Mari-Jo Fraser, Alan Gibson, David "Doc" Kuechle, David Mathews, Brian Morrison, Rick Park

 

The 2021 HOF Class includes:​

Mark Aspelund, Nate Binz, Adrian Brockway, Deryck Christensen, Sean Prouty, DJ Penick Jr.

 

Congratulations to each one of these deserving players!  In addition to being premier athletes, they are great ambassadors for the PSSBL and the game of baseball.  They join 52 players previously inducted into “The Hall”.
