2021 PSSBL Tryouts - What To Expect

In order to adhere to state and local guidelines and limit close contact among those in attendance, a special plan is being put in place for the 2021 PSSBL tryouts. 

Highlights of this plan:

  • Tryout players will attend tryouts in small groups (8-12 players) by appointment with additional COVID-19 mitigation practices in place.  

  • Scouting can be conducted in person or remotely via streaming video via Facebook Live.  Video clips will be made available for additional viewing later on the website.

  • Tryouts will be conducted with ~6-8 onsite staff at any given time.

When You Arrive:

  • Plan to arrive at the field no more than 15 min before your appointment time.  If you arrive earlier than that, please remain in your car until then.  

  • When you arrive at the tryout location, your bib number (including safety pins) will be laid out at the PSSBL tent and labeled with your name.  

  • Pick up your bib and pin it on to the front of your shirt.   You are encouraged to use the provided hand sanitizer before leaving the registration area.   

  • A staff member will take your picture that will be posted on your draft profile on the league website.

  • After this you’ll have a few minutes to do whatever stretching or warmup you need.  

  • Players should maintain at least 6 ft separation from others to the extent possible.

  • In adherence to state and local guidelines, all players, scouts and tryout staff are required to wear facemasks at all times.

Conducting the tryout:

At the beginning of each tryout session, players will be given a new or sanitized ball to warm up with for ~10 min.   While players are warming up, onsite staff will use sanitizer wipes to sanitize the balls that will be used for the tryouts.

Similar to most tryouts, the sessions will be broken into four phases:  

  • Infield:  ~20 min  

1-2 tryout players will be stationed at each infield position including catcher if there is one.  One onsite staff member will hit in a typical infield drill. 

  • Outfield:   ~15 min  

Players will be stationed at the three OF positions and will be hit a combination of fly balls and line drives/grounders.  The remaining players will take the throws and/or serve as cutoff.   These players will rotate into the OF after the first group.

  • Hitting:  ~40 min

One player will hit and one will be on deck at any given time - the remaining players will shag in the infield or outfield and will rotate into the on deck position as players finish their turn batting.  One staff member will throw BP.  Each player will get 2 bunts and 8-10 swings.  

  • Pitching:  ~20 min

Players who do not wish to tryout as pitchers should leave the field promptly.  Players who wish to tryout as pitchers, will throw ~15 pitches in the bullpen with a new or freshly sanitized ball.  If there is a tryout catcher with his own gear, they will catch.  If not a staff member will catch. 

During the tryout activities, there will be at least two staff members shooting video of the tryouts.  One will be streaming video live and the other(s) will be shooting short clips to post on the tryout player profile pages.
