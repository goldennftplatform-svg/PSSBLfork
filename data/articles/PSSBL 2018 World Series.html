Congratulations to three PSSBL-based teams who won World Series Championships this fall. The Puget Sound Reds dominated the Nor Cal Knights 19-5 in the 35 & Over Cactus Division final to claim a MSBL title in Phoenix.

In Ft. Myers, Florida the 70+ Washington Titans compiled a perfect 8-0 record for the week defeating the Sacramento Solons 12-4 in the title game to take the Roy Hobbs AAAA crown.

 

The 60+ Washington Titans won a pair of back-to-back doubleheaders during the playoff run to claim the AAA Roy Hobbs championship.  The 60's beat the Philadelphia Cutters 6-3 in the final to win it all. 

Also in Ft. Myers half a dozen PSSBL players were instrumental in bringing home the hardware for the Border City Brewers who swept through the Roy Hobbs 53 & Over AAAA Division.  

Two other PSSBL-based teams made it all the way to the title game, but fell a bit short and wound up as runners-up. The 3n2 Rangers lost to St. Louis Gameface 5-4 to finish 2nd in the MSBL 55+ Central Division in Phoenix. The 50+ version of the 3n2 Rangers missed out on a 2018 MSBL championship, losing out to a strong Raleigh Braves team.

A number of other PSSBL-based teams fared well enough in their respective round robin games to make it to the playoffs in Arizona and Florida.  All in all a great showing by the PSSBL at the national level in 2018!
