ENVISION NORTHWEST, piloted by Greg Bradford and Greg Werre, recently took home the hardware in the 60 & Over Division at MSBL's Kickoff Classic in Las Vegas.

The team forged a 4-1 record during the tourney, ultimately outscoring their opponents 57-31.  Envision Northwest avenged their only pool play loss - to the Mustangs Baseball Club - in the championship game, shutting out the Mustangs 7-0.

   Envision NW

Team MVP Michael Smith tossed a complete game shutout against the Mustangs to secure the title.  Smith was also an offensive weapon during the entire competition, batting .444 with 9 Runs, 9 RBI, and 5 stolen bases.

Other stars included PSSBL Hall of Famer Larry Davis and Rocky Nordness, who hit an incredible .688.  Doug Springford threw a complete game win against the Las Vegas 60's in pool play, surrendering just 8 Hits and 2 Unearned Runs.  Greg Werre caught 4 of the 5 games, including the championship shutout.

Envision Northwest manager Greg Bradford stated "I couldn't be prouder of my team than I am for continuing to play hard and finishing strong to win the championship".
