PSSBL Career Stats Explorer

The PSSBL career stats explorer allows you to search through the historical records of the league and identify statistical leaders. This article provides instructions for its use and identifies some of the limitations of the tool.

 

Disclaimer

Please understand that there are significant limitations on the completeness and accuracy of information available in the league statistical database.The stat database only goes back to the 2008 season, whereas the league was founded in 1989. Therefore, over 20 years of data are not included in the results returned by the tool. The career statistics of many players are thus incomplete and some prominent players from the early days of the league may not be included at all.

There are many other sources of incompleteness and inaccuracy. Some teams have been far less inclined than others to enter stats into the system over the years. Scoring standards may also vary significantly from team to team and division to division. Certain stats, like Wild Pitches and Intentional Base on Balls are rarely entered on a consistent basis. And, of course, some players are more passionate about campaigning for favorable scoring decisions. Also remember that the competition level among the different divisions varies significantly, so direct comparisons among players must be put into an appropriate perspective.

That all being said, we hope you find the tool interesting and entertaining.

 

Instructions and Explanations

To view a statistical table, first select the desired parameters and then click the "Submit" button to view the results. There are a wide number of options for filtering the statistics and a large number of viewable stats. The main types of searches are as follows:

Search Types

Career Leaders - This will display the combined records for all years in the database.

Single Season Records - This will return a sorted list of leaders based on the best statistical totals from any single season. When selecting this option you will also be presented with the opportunity to  choose how to restrict the results:

Restrict to All Teams: Single season records will include stats for all games played by an individual, including multiple teams they are members of and taxi pool assignments.

Restrict to Single Team: Single season records will be restricted to those accumulated while playing for a particular team in that season.

Specific Year - This search type will let you identify league-wide leaders for a specific calendar year. You can not further restrict the scope of these searches, since team-wide and division-wide statistics are already available on the main team and division pages of the website, respectively.

Last Five Seasons - This is equivalent to the career search type, except that the results are limited to the past five seasons, inclusive of the current season. This will help you identify recent movers and shakers.

Search Scope

League Wide - This includes all games that a player has participated in, regardless of division.

Division - The results returned will only include statistics from games played within the specified division. It does include stats from taxi pool assignments played within the division.

Franchise - You can view results that are limited to a specified team franchise. To select the franchise, select any representative year, division, and team name that identifies the franchise. Results will include all teams and seasons associated with that same franchise.

Search Category

You can choose to view either Batting or Pitching statistics. Each has a large number of possible stats to choose from using the right-most drop down box.

Additional Notes

Update Interval - Compiling these statistical lists requires sifting through hundreds of thousands of records and doing all of this in real time would be a demanding task that could severely stress our server resources. Therefore, a nightly background task runs on the server that does a lot of the preliminary compilation work. It may therefore take up to 24 hours for newly reported game statistics to show up in the career tallies. This allows the searches to respond very quickly without putting undo stress on the server processors.

Qualifying Criteria - Only the top 100 leaders in any category can be viewed. You can choose to view these 25 at a time, 50 at a time, or all on one page, as you prefer. In most cases, there are no qualifying restrictions for players to be listed, but for statistics that involve averages, minimum qualifying requirements must be met. These are as follows

Batting Qualification Criteria: When viewing leaders in batting average, on base percentage, slugging percentage, or OBS, qualifying players must have accumulated a minimum of 200 at bats (career stats), 100 at bats (franchise leaders), or 25 at bats (single season records).

Pitching Qualification Criteria: When viewing leaders in earned run average, WHIP (walks plus hits per inning pitched), walks per nine innings, or strikeouts per nine innings, qualifying players must have thrown a minimum of 100 innings (career stats), 25 innings (franchise leaders), or 25 innings (single season records).
