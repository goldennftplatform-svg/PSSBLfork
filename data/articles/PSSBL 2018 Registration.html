Welcome to the 30th season of the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League!  Online registration for the 2018 PSSBL baseball season is now open.

If you are a returning player, log in to your account first, click on your name to get the dropdown menu, select “Registration” and follow the prompts. "Buddy Picks" should respond to a registration email sent directly to you by your GM.

New tryout players are strongly encouraged to sign up online using the Sign Up button in the "Register for Tryouts" box adjacent to this article.  Traditional registration for new players entering the draft is also possible at the check-in table on tryout days. The tryouts are set for March 4th and March 11th at Interlake High School in Bellevue, March 17th at Bellevue College and March 24th at Russell Road Park in Kent.

As you proceed through the online registration process, make sure to read the 2018 PSSBL Refund Policy and the PSSBL waiver which you sign electronically.  If you encounter technical issues as you register, contact support@pssbl.com.

Thanks to Web Developer Rich Bonny for preparing the online 2018 season with an assist from Michael Willard, Jake Liddil and Jason Yormark.  Have a great 2018 season - Play ball!
