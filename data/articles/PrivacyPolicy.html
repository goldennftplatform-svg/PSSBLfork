Personal Identification Information

The information collected during the registration process will not be shared with or sold by the PSSBL to any commercial business, organization, entity or person, except for the purposes of enrollment in the national organization we are affiliated with, the Men's Senior Baseball League/Men's Adult Baseball League (MSBL/MABL)of New York.

Enrollment in the national organization is mandatory for all members of the PSSBL in order to obtain the liability insurance coverage necessary to play baseball on all of the facilities we use and to participate in MSBL/MABL-sponsored tournaments. PSSBL members may receive discount offers on insurance, travel, car rentals and baseball equipment available at the MSBLSportsStore through membership in the MSBL/ MABL.

The PSSBL web site will contain rosters of all teams which will show members names, but no other personal identification will be shown unless the league member agrees to add a phone number or e-mail address to the on-line roster.

Credit Card Information

The PSSBL will not collect or store any credit card information as part of the registration process. Credit card payment of annual league dues will be processed through VeriSign, utilizing Secure Socket Layer (SSL) encryption to ensure that your information is protected.
