How do I send a buddy pick invitation?

To send a buddy pick invitation, you’ll need to be signed in to the website with General Manager permissions for the team.  

Once you’re logged in, navigate to your team page, select the “Roster” tab, and then click the “Manage” button to open the Roster Management screen.

Click the “Invite Buddy Picks” button and on the following screen input the player’s name and email address and click the button to send the email invitation.   

Important - be sure you have the correct email address otherwise the player won’t receive the email.   If you are inviting a player who is already in the league, be sure you are using the email address s/he used to register.

On this screen, you can also view all of the buddy pick invitations you’ve sent for the current season and the status of each.

 

I sent a buddy pick invitation, but the player didn’t receive it (or lost it) - what do I do?

If the player never received the email, double check you have the correct email address (many times it’s just a typo) and also have the player check their junk mail / spam folder.

If you have confirmed both of these things or if the player received the email, but lost it - you can resend the invitation.    

Navigate back to the screen where you sent the original invitation, re-enter the player’s name and email address in the buddy pick invitation screen and click the button to send the invitation.  The website will display a notification that an invitation has already been sent to this email address with the option to re-send the invitation. Select Yes to re-send the invitation.

Note - in order to reduce the chance for errors, this functionality can only be used if an invitation hasn’t been sent to the same player for 12 hours.  If, for some reason, you urgently need an invitation resent inside of this 12 hour window, contact support@pssbl.com.

 

I sent a buddy pick invitation and the player accepted it, but he’s still not on my roster - what happened?

Registering for a new team as a buddy pick is a two-step process.  

1) Accept the buddy pick invitation  

2) Register for the season

If your player has a accepted the buddy pick invitation, but is not appearing on your roster, most likely they haven’t completed the second step.  Details for registering as a buddy pick can be found here:  https://pssbl.com/#!/articles/faq-registration#NewBP


 

I’m short on players for tonight’s game - can I invite my friend (or neighbor / brother / sister / Ken Griffey Jr) to play?

Only registered and paid PSSBL league members who meet all the eligibility criteria for the division may appear in a PSSBL game.  Playing an ineligible player is strictly prohibited and may subject the GM to significant penalties.

The appropriate avenue for getting additional players to fill in for a game is to submit a Taxi Pool request.  For more information, click here to review the Taxi Pool policy.   

As a general recommendation, if you have fewer than 11 players confirmed for your game you should consider requesting player(s) from the Taxi Pool to help cover you in the event of an injury or last minute cancellation.  For best results, try to submit your request at least 48 hours in advance of your game.


 

I just found out I won’t have enough players for my game that starts in three hours - what do I do?

If you haven’t already, the first step is to submit a Taxi Pool request.     

Next, immediately contact your Division Commissioner and the league Player Agent and request their help try to find a player on short notice. 


 

How do I send a Taxi Pool request?

To send a Taxi Pool request, you must be signed into the website with GM level permissions on the team.

Navigate to your team schedule, find the game for which you need additional players and use the link in the “Result” column to open the game details page.  On the game details page, select the link to “Request Taxi Players”, then fill in the request details and submit the request.

Once you submit the request, it will be sent to 25 recipients for each Taxi Player requested.  The pool of players who receive the request will be selected randomly by the system based on a defined set of criteria.   

Within that group, players will be assigned to play for your team on a first come, first served basis.

 

I submitted a Taxi Pool request, but nobody responded - what do I do?

If you have not received any responses (or you have not received enough responses) to a Taxi Pool request you submitted, you can choose to expand the request.    

To expand an existing request, navigate to the game details page just like you did when you submitted the request originally.   In the “Request Taxi Players” section, select the button to “Expand” the request. This will send the request to an additional 25 recipients for each open/infilled Taxi Player requested.

If your game is less than 24 hours away and you still don’t have enough players confirmed, (AFTER following the steps above) contact your Division Commissioner and the league Player Agent and request their help try to find a player on short notice.


 

Can other players on my team enter stats (or submit taxi pool requests / etc...)?

Yes - and as the GM of the team, you can assign them these permissions from the Roster Management page.  To assign permissions for your team, you must be signed into the website with GM level permissions on the team.

Once signed in, navigate to your team’s page on the website, select the “Roster” tab and then the “Manage” button to open the Roster Management screen.  Locate the player to whom you would like to assign permissions and then click the “Edit Player” button on the row next to their name to open the player’s profile page.  

On the right hand side of the player’s profile page, select the permissions level you wish to assign to the player and then click the “Save Permissions” button to commit the change.  The box next to each permissions level provides detail about what functions the player will be able to perform.


 

Someone else is taking over as General Manager.   How do we make that change?

The change on the website needs to be made by an administrator.  Please email  support@pssbl.com along with the league president and your division commissioner to request this change.
 

 

How does the online trade tool work?

Beginning with the 2019 PSSBL season, all trades are completed using the online trade tool.   

- Gone are the days of processing trades with the old paper form and trying to find a fax machine to send it to the commissioner.

- Gone are the days of forgetting who’s draft pick you traded for after 4 beers.

- Gone are the days of not remembering which kind of bourbon you owe that other GM for the 9th pick in the 2nd round. 

Welcome to the 21st Century, where all that is just a few clicks away!

To record a trade, you must be signed into the website with GM level permissions on the team.

Once signed in, navigate to your division page and select the trades tab.
From there, click on the button to log a new trade.   On the resulting page, fill in the appropriate fields and click submit.   

Important:  when you fill in the trade details, keep in mind you are identifying what each team will RECEIVE in the trade.


Once you click submit, it’s recommended you notify the other GM, your Division Commissioner and the Player Agent so they can review and approve accordingly.   Each should notify all of the others as they approve as well.
If there is a need to modify the trade details or if the trade is rejected, email support@pssbl.com with the appropriate details.

Trade Guidelines:  

A few guidelines to remember - if you have any questions about these guidelines, please consult your Division Commissioner and/or the Player Agent.
- Any player being traded must agree to the trade.
- Trades may not include monetary compensation.
- Trades can only be executed between teams in the same division.
- In most cases, there is no need to trade a player for no compensation (just release the player and he can be added to the other team as a buddy pick).
- Trades for ambiguous compensation (i.e. - “player to be named later” or “future considerations”) will be rejected.  Figure out the terms of trade before you submit it.
- If you are trading for a draft pick, be as specific as possible about the pick (i.e. - year, round, pick number, etc...).
- In divisions with limitations around buddy picks and waiver selections, those things are not valid as trade compensation.
 

Limitations of the Trade Tool:  

This is the first iteration of the tool and there are some limitations (most of which will be addressed with future enhancements).
- For now, the tool only has the ability to process approvals - so you can’t reject a trade (yet).  You also can’t edit a trade that’s been submitted. The recommended practice is to agree on the terms of the trade outside of the tool and just use the tool to record the trade you’ve agreed to.   If you need to cancel or modify a trade, contact support@pssbl.com for assistance.
- If you trade a player from one team to another, the tool will not automatically move them to the new team.  Once the trade is completed, contact support@pssbl.com to help get the player on the correct roster
- The tool only supports trades between 2 teams.  If you’re trying to become executive of the year by engineering an epic 4-team trade... sorry, you’ll have to submit multiple transactions in the system (honestly, I doubt we’ll ever get to the point where the tool supports multiple-team trades).
