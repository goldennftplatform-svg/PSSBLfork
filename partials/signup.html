After the draft:

If the draft has already concluded, most teams will already have full rosters but there are still a couple possible ways to get involved for the current season.

Taxi Pool

The Taxi Pool is a pool (or sub-group) of PSSBL members who wish to be contacted from time to time to play for teams requesting additional players for a particular game.

The purposes of the Taxi Pool are: a) to ensure that no game is ever forfeited due to a lack of available players on a team, and b) to provide an opportunity for the League's members to play in extra games. More details can be found here: Taxi Pool Policy

All members of the PSSBL are eligible to participate in the Taxi Pool, so all that is required is to register and pay the PSSBL base dues of {{season.baseDues}}. Taxi pool usage is based on the needs of the teams, so there is no guaranteed number of games a player will be invited to play; however we do prioritize “Taxi-Pool” only players in the selection. Follow the instructions at the FAQ (How / when can I sign up?) to register for the taxi pool.

Free Agents

While all teams already have rosters of 15 or more players by the conclusion of the draft, situations often arise during the season which cause teams to need additional players.

The league player agent maintains a list of players who wish to be considered as late adds and distributes this list to all teams periodically throughout the season. Every effort will be made to help players find a team, but understand that it will be on a best effort basis and depends largely on the needs of the teams.

There is no cost to be on the Free Agent list. If you end up placed on a team, you will pay a prorated portion of the player dues based on the number of games remaining in the season (you must be fully registered and paid before you appear in any game). In order to be eligible for the playoffs, you would need to be on the roster of a team no later than midnight on {{season.playoffCutoffDate}}.

Please do not submit a request if you already have an account on PSSBL.com. Anyone who has played in the league or tried out for a team since 2006 is likely to already have an account.
