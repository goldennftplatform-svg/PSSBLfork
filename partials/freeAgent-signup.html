WAIVER WIRE DESCRIPTION:

The Waiver Wire is intended for players who currently are NOT rostered on a team. If you are currently on a Team in the PCBL, do not proceed.
Waiver Wire - While all teams already have rosters of 15 or more players by the conclusion of the draft, situations often arise during the season which cause teams to need additional players. The league player agent maintains a list of players who wish to be considered as late adds and distributes this list to all teams periodically throughout the season. Every effort will be made to help players find a team, but understand that it will be on a best effort basis and depends largely on the needs of the teams. There is no cost to be on the Waiver Wire list. If you end up placed on a team, you will pay a prorated portion of the player dues based on the number of games remaining in the season (you must be fully registered and paid before you appear in any game). In order to be eligible for the playoffs, you would need to be on the roster of a team no later than midnight on July 15th.
